To connect, you add the Plytix MCP to your AI application as a remote MCP server, then sign in to Plytix with OAuth to authorize it. This guide has step-by-step instructions for Claude and ChatGPT, and a check to run once you're connected.

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have the following:

- Plytix MCP access turned on for your Plytix account. To request it, contact your account manager.
- An AI application that supports remote MCP servers and OAuth sign-in, such as Claude or ChatGPT. Your application's plan or workspace settings decide whether you can add a custom connection, and a workspace administrator may need to add or approve it first. Check the requirements for your application in the tabs below.

You'll need the following connection details:

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Server URL | `https://pim.plytix.com/mcp` |
| Authentication | OAuth |

The Plytix MCP is separate from the V3 API, so you don't need V3 or a [V3 access token](/reference/v3/authentication) to connect. The OAuth sign-in authorizes your AI application, separately from the access tokens in the **API v3** tab.

Pick your AI application and follow the steps to add the server, authorize it, and turn it on in a conversation.

## Verify the connection

Follow the steps below to confirm that your AI application found the Plytix tools and can read your account.

1. **Check** that your application lists the Plytix tools. Claude shows them in the connector's settings, and ChatGPT on the plugin's details page.
2. **Ask** what the connection can do:

   ```text
   What can you do with my Plytix account through this connection?
   ```

   A working connection answers with the capabilities in this release. This confirms that the tools were found, but not that your assistant can read your data.

3. **Run** a small read that you can check yourself, such as the first prompt in [Example workflows](/docs/guides/platform-mcp/example-workflows#find-a-product):

   ```text
   Using Plytix, find the product with SKU GF-Basket and show its name and description.
   ```

   Check that your assistant called a Plytix tool, then compare the answer against the product in Plytix. If the values match, the connection works and your account is reachable. If the answer has no specifics, your assistant probably answered without calling Plytix. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/guides/platform-mcp/troubleshooting).
