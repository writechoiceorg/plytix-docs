Add the Plytix MCP as a remote MCP server in your AI application, then sign in to Plytix with OAuth. Follow the instructions for Claude or ChatGPT below, then verify the connection.

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have the following:

- Plytix MCP access turned on for your Plytix account. To request it, contact your account manager.
- An AI application that supports remote MCP servers and OAuth sign-in, such as Claude or ChatGPT. Your subscription plan and workspace settings determine whether you can add a custom connection. A workspace administrator may need to add or approve the connection first. Check the requirements for your application in the tabs below.

You'll need the following connection details:

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Server URL | `https://pim.plytix.com/mcp` |
| Authentication | OAuth |

You don't need V3 API beta access or a V3 access token to use the Plytix MCP. Sign in to Plytix with OAuth when your AI application prompts you.

## Add the Plytix MCP to your AI application

Select your AI application and follow the steps to add the server, authorize the connection, and enable it in a conversation.

    Custom connectors work in Claude on the web and in Claude Desktop, on every plan. On the Free plan, you can add one custom connector.

    **On a Team or Enterprise plan**, the connector is set up once for the whole organization, then each member signs in.

    **Organization setup:** An owner or another user with connector-management permissions adds Plytix. On Team, Owners and Primary Owners can add it. On Enterprise, Owners, Primary Owners, and custom roles with Manage access to Libraries can add it.

    5. **Select** **Sign in now** under **Authentication**, keep the detected **OAuth client** setting, and click **Add**.

    **Member sign-in:** Each member connects with their own Plytix account.

    1. **Open** **Customize** > **Connectors**, select **Plytix**, and click **Connect**.
    2. **Sign in** to your own Plytix account in the window that opens. Review the access that Claude requests, then approve it.
    3. **Turn on** the connector in a conversation: click the **+** button in the lower left of the chat, click **Connectors**, and turn on the **Plytix** toggle.

    **On a Free, Pro, or Max plan**, add the connector yourself:

    1. **Open** **Customize** > **Connectors** in Claude.
    2. **Click** **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.

On a Team or Enterprise plan, owners remove the organization's connector in **Organization settings** > **Connectors**.

## Verify the connection

Follow the steps below to confirm that your AI application found the Plytix tools and can read your account.

1. **Check** the tool list in your AI application's connection settings to see which tools were discovered. Claude shows them in the connector's settings, and ChatGPT on the plugin's details page.
2. **Run** a small read that you can check yourself, the first prompt in [Example workflows](/mcp/example-workflows#find-a-product). Replace `YOUR-SKU` with a SKU that exists in your account, then ask:

   ```text
   Using Plytix, find the product with SKU YOUR-SKU and show its SKU, name, and description. Don't change anything.
   ```

   Check that your assistant called a Plytix tool, then compare the answer against the product in Plytix. If the values match, the connection works and your account is reachable. If the answer has no specifics, your assistant probably answered without calling Plytix. See [Troubleshooting](/mcp/troubleshooting).

An answer that only describes the available capabilities doesn't confirm a successful connection, so always finish with the product lookup.
