## November 9, 2026

Beta release.

- **Added:** Tools to read and change your products, your assets, and the catalog structure around them, such as categories, lists, and families. See [Capabilities and permissions](/mcp/tools).
- **Usage notes:** The initial beta supports product data, assets, and the catalog structures described in [Capabilities and permissions](/mcp/tools). Channel management is outside the supported scope.
