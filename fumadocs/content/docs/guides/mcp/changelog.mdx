## November 6, 2026

Beta release.

- **Added:** Tools to read and change your products, your assets, and the catalog structure around them, such as categories, lists, and families. See [Capabilities and permissions](/docs/guides/platform-mcp/capabilities-and-permissions).
- **Limitations:** The beta covers your product and asset data only. Other parts of Plytix, such as channels, aren't available through the Plytix MCP.
