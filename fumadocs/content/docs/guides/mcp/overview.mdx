## What is the Plytix MCP?

The Plytix MCP is a server that lets an AI application, such as an AI assistant or agent, work with the data in your Plytix account. It uses the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard that lets AI applications discover and call tools exposed by external services.

After you connect, your AI application discovers the available Plytix tools. Ask your assistant a question or describe a task in natural language. When needed, specify that it should use the Plytix connection.

The Plytix MCP provides tools for working with your catalog through an AI assistant. See [Capabilities and permissions](/mcp/tools) for the available operations. Tools may be added, updated, or removed over time, with changes recorded in the [changelog](/mcp/changelog).

## How access works

You sign in to Plytix with OAuth to authorize your AI application. The available tools and the access granted to the connection determine what your assistant can do.

Some AI applications also let you control individual tools, for example by asking for your approval before a tool runs or by turning a tool off. These controls belong to the AI application, so they differ from one application to another. Some tools can change your catalog, so try the [example workflows](/mcp/example-workflows) on a test account first.
