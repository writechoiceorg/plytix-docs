## What is the Plytix MCP?

The Plytix MCP is a server that lets an AI application, such as an AI assistant or agent, work with the data in your Plytix account. It uses the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard that lets AI applications discover and call tools exposed by external services.

Once you connect it, your assistant finds the Plytix tools on its own and calls them when your request needs your account's data. You ask in plain language, and you don't call tools by name.

The Plytix MCP isn't an API version, and it doesn't give your assistant every endpoint in the [V3 API](/reference/v3). It offers the tools summarized in [Capabilities and permissions](/docs/guides/platform-mcp/capabilities-and-permissions), and that list grows from release to release.

It's also separate from the [Documentation MCP](/docs/guides/documentation-mcp), which reads these docs, needs no account, and can't see your data. Connecting one doesn't connect the other.

## How access works

The Plytix MCP signs in with OAuth, so you authorize your AI application in the browser instead of pasting a token. What your assistant can do depends on the tools in the current release and the access you grant when you authorize the connection.

Some AI applications also let you control individual tools, for example by asking for your approval before a tool runs or by turning a tool off. These controls belong to the AI application, so they differ from one application to another. Some tools can change your catalog, so try the [example workflows](/docs/guides/platform-mcp/example-workflows) on a test account first.
