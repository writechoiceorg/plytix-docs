          Fetch a single product by its Plytix product `id`. This always returns
          the full product record. It doesn't support `_fields` filtering the
          way [Search-Products](/docs/reference/v3/products/search_products)
          does.


          The `identifier` must be the product's Plytix ID, not its SKU, even
          though SKU is the natural key for most catalogs. If you only have a
          SKU, look it up first with
          [Search-Products](/docs/reference/v3/products/search_products) (filter
          on `sku=<value>` with `_fields=id`), then use the returned `id` here.
