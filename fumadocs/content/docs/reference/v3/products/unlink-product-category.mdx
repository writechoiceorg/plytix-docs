  structuredData:

    contents:
      - content: >-
          Unlink one category from a product. This removes only the association:
          the category and the product's other categories stay. A successful
          unlink returns `204` with an empty body, and a category that isn't
          linked to the product returns `404`.


          To delete the category itself from your account, use [Delete a product
          category](/reference/v3/product-categories/delete-product-category).
          That can also delete its subcategories.
