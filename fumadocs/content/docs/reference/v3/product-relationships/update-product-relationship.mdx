    contents:
      - content: |-
          Update the related products on an existing link, most often to change a `quantity`. The body is `{"related_products": [{"product_id": "<id>", "quantity": 5}]}`, the same entry shape as [Add related products](/reference/v3/product-relationships/add-related-products).

          Send the full array you want the link to end up with. That makes this the way to drop one related product from a multi-product link: PATCH the array without it. To remove the link and every product under it at once, use [Remove a product relationship](/reference/v3/product-relationships/remove-product-relationship).

          The response carries the related product's ID twice per entry, as both `product_id` and `related_product_id` with the same value. Either one identifies the product on the other end of the link.
