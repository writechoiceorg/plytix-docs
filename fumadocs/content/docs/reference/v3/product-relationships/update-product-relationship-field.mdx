      - content: |-
          Update a single field on one of a product's relationship links. `path` is the field name, and the request body is that field's new value on its own, not an object wrapping it.

          The only part of a link meant to change is its `related_products` array, and [Update a product relationship](/reference/v3/product-relationships/update-product-relationship) is the confirmed way to change it, taking `{"related_products": [...]}` as a partial object. Use that endpoint instead. `relationship_id` and `relationship_label` identify the link and its type, so rewriting them here isn't a supported way to move a product to another relationship type; link it under the new type with [Add related products](/reference/v3/product-relationships/add-related-products), then unlink the old one with [Remove a product relationship](/reference/v3/product-relationships/remove-product-relationship).
