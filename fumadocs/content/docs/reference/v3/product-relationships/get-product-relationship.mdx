  structuredData:
    headings: []
    contents:
      - content: >-
          Fetch one of a product's relationship links by the ID of its
          relationship type. Returns the flat record `{relationship_id,
          relationship_label, product_id}`.


          Both path segments take Plytix IDs: `product_id` is the product's
          `id`, not its SKU, and `relationship_id` is the ID of the relationship
          type, from [Search
          relationships](/reference/v3/relationships/search-relationships).


          This tells you which relationship type the product takes part in, not
          which products sit on the other end of it. To see the products a link
          points to, use [Search related
          products](/reference/v3/related-products/search-related-products) with
          `?product_id=<id>`.
