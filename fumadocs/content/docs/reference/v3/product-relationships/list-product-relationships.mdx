      - content: |-
          List every relationship link a product currently has. Each entry is flat (`{relationship_id, relationship_label, product_id}`) and a product with no links returns an empty `data` array.

          Results are scoped by the `product_id` in the URL, so this needs none of the filtering that [Search product relationships](/reference/v3/product-relationships/search-product-relationships) requires. `product_id` is the product's Plytix `id`, not its SKU.

          The entries name the relationship types the product takes part in, not the products on the other end of them, there's no `related_product_id` in this shape. For that, use [Search related products](/reference/v3/related-products/search-related-products) with `?product_id=<id>`.
