          Link related products to a product under the relationship type named
          in the URL, rather than passing `relationship_id` in the body.


          The body carries the related products as `related_products`, an array
          of `{"product_id": "<id>", "quantity": N}` entries. The nested field
          is `product_id`; `id` is rejected.


          [Add related
          products](/reference/v3/product-relationships/add-related-products) is
          the confirmed form of this operation, taking the same
          `related_products` array plus `relationship_id` in the body, and
          returning each `related_product_id` it created. Prefer it unless you
          specifically want the relationship type pinned in the URL.
