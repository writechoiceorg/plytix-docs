    contents:
      - content: >-
          Permanently delete a product category by its Plytix `id`. A successful
          delete returns `204` with an empty body.


          > **Warning:** Deleting a parent category can delete its whole
          subtree. In Plytix's controlled Dev test (October 2026), deleting a
          parent category also deleted its child and grandchild categories, and
          the test product lost its associations with them. The product itself
          stayed, and an unrelated category was unaffected. The effect on many
          products, and on production, hasn't been tested yet, so check the
          category's subcategories before you delete it.


          Deleting a category isn't the way to take one product out of it. To
          remove a single product's association and keep the category, use
          [Unlink a category from a
          product](/reference/v3/products/unlink-product-category).
