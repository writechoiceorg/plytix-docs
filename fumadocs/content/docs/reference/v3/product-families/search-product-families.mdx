      - content: >-
          Returns the product families in your account, one page at a time. Each
          record holds the family's `id`, its `name`, and its timestamps.


          Use it to find a family's `id` before you assign products to the
          family with `product_family_id`, or before you work with its
          attributes.


          Filters use the same syntax as every V3 search, for example
          `name[contains:ignorecase]=aprons`. To sort by name, send
          `_sort_by=name`.


          To get each family's attributes, with each attribute's inheritance
          `level`, add `_fields=family_attributes`. They come back only when you
          name them in `_fields`. You can also list one family's attributes with
          [List a product family's
          attributes](/reference/v3/family-attributes/list-family-attributes).
