          Renames a product category, moves it under another parent, or both. Send only the fields you want to change: `name`, `parent_id`, or both. A successful update returns `204` with an empty body. To see the result, read the category back.

          Renaming a category also changes its `slug` and the last name in its `path`. Moving it with `parent_id` changes its `path` to the new chain of names.

          The body accepts only `name` and `parent_id`. To change which products are filed under the category, link and unlink them on the product instead. See [Safely remove categories, assets, or relationships](/docs/guides/safely-removing-categories-assets-or-relationships#remove-a-category-from-a-product) and [Update and delete records](/docs/reference/v3/updates-and-deletes).
