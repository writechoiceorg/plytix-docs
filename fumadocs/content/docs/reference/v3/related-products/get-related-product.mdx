          Fetch a single product-to-product link: the record connecting
          `product_id` to `related_product_id` under `relationship_id`.


          > **Warning:** This endpoint returns `404 NotFoundError` for links
          that do exist, the same underlying problem that makes its [collection
          form](/reference/v3/related-products/list-related-products) come back
          empty. Don't use it to check whether two products are linked. Use
          [Search related
          products](/reference/v3/related-products/search-related-products) with
          `?product_id=<id>` instead.


          All three path segments take Plytix IDs, not SKUs.
