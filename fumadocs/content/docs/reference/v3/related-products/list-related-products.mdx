          List the products linked to one product under a single relationship type.

          > **Warning:** This endpoint returns an empty `data` array even when the relationship has live links. Use [Search related products](/reference/v3/related-products/search-related-products) with `?product_id=<id>` instead.

          Both path segments take Plytix IDs: `product_id` is the product's `id`, not its SKU, and `relationship_id` is the ID of the relationship type, from [Search relationships](/reference/v3/relationships/search-relationships). To see which relationship types a product takes part in, use [List a product's relationships](/reference/v3/product-relationships/list-product-relationships).
