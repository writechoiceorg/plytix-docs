          Changes the `level` of an attribute's link to the product family. Send
          the new level, for example `{"level": "variant_level"}`. A successful
          change returns `204` with an empty body.


          Changing a level needs automatic inheritance on your account. Without
          it, the request returns `403` with "Automatic inheritance is not
          available for this account", whatever level you send. If you get this
          response, ask your Plytix contact whether inheritance is available for
          your account. See [Update and delete
          records](/docs/reference/v3/updates-and-deletes).
