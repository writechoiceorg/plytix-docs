          Deletes one asset from your account. A successful delete returns `204` with an empty body, and a later request for the asset returns `404`.

          Before you delete an asset, check that no product uses it as its thumbnail (`thumbnail_id`) or as a value of a media or media gallery attribute.

          To take an asset out of an asset category without deleting it, use [Unlink a category from an asset](/reference/v3/assets/delete-asset-field). To detach an asset from a product, change the product instead: see [Safely remove categories, assets, or relationships](/docs/guides/safely-removing-categories-assets-or-relationships#connect-other-assets-to-a-product) and [Update and delete records](/reference/v3/updates-and-deletes).
