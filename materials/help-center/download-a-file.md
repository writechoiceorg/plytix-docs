# Downloading Assets

## How to download your assets from Plytix

There are two ways to download digital files in Plytix: individually and in bulk. 

 

[Downloading Individual Assets](#Individual)

[Downloading Assets in Bulk](#bulk)

_*Skip to a section in this article by clicking on the links above_

---

### Downloading Individual Assets

Individual asset downloads will download directly to your computer.

You can download individual assets from the: 

[Asset Overview](#asset-overview)

[Asset Detail Page](#asset-details)

[Product Detail Page](#product-page)

#### From the Asset Overview

You can download individual asset files directly from the "**All assets**" page. 
1. Use the filters or search bar to find the asset you want to download
2. If in table view, hover over the row of the asset 
3. Click the options icon with three dots (see image below)
4. Choose the option "Download asset"
5. Then the asset will download directly to your browser

![2. Download asset individually](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Download%20a%20file/2.%20Download%20asset%20individually.png?width=670&height=356&name=2.%20Download%20asset%20individually.png)

You can also download assets from the more visual grid view. To do this, hover over the image you want to select, and click on '**Download asset.**'

![3. Download asset in grid view](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Download%20a%20file/3.%20Download%20asset%20in%20grid%20view.png?width=670&height=357&name=3.%20Download%20asset%20in%20grid%20view.png)

#### Downloading from the Asset Detail Page

Download assets directly from the asset detail page by clicking the download icon in the top right corner.

![4. Download individual assets](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Download%20a%20file/4.%20Download%20individual%20assets.png?width=670&height=357&name=4.%20Download%20individual%20assets.png)

#### Downloading assets from a product detail page

You can also download assets that are linked to a product directly from the product details page.
1. From the product detail click on the "Assets" tab
2. If in table view, hover over the row of the asset you want to download
If in grid view, hover over the asset you want to download
3. Click the download icon

![5. Download from product attr](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Download%20a%20file/5.%20Download%20from%20product%20attr.png?width=670&height=357&name=5.%20Download%20from%20product%20attr.png)

### How to download assets in bulk

Bulk downloads will deliver you files in a ZIP folder and/or in a CSV format via URLs. 

You can download assets in bulk from the:

[Asset overview](#bulk-overview)

[Product overview table](#bulk-product-overview)

 

#### From the Assets Overview

Downloading files from the Assets Overview allows you to select exactly which files you want to download.

To do this: 
1. Go to "Assets" in the top navigation menu
2. Select "All assets"
3. Select the assets you want to download from the asset overview
4. Click the "Export" button that appears above the product table

![8. Bulk download from assets](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Download%20a%20file/8.%20Bulk%20download%20from%20assets.png?width=644&height=343&name=8.%20Bulk%20download%20from%20assets.png)

5. Name the file
6. Choose which format to download as: 
**CSV** or **XLSX** to receive the asset files as URLs (and also in a folder)
**Asset files** to get them downloaded into a ZIP folder

Then, click '**Next'**

![9. Export assets format](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Download%20a%20file/9.%20Export%20assets%20format.png?width=670&height=357&name=9.%20Export%20assets%20format.png)

7. If downloading as a CSV or XLSX, you can also select any additional attributes you want to show in the sheet

![10. Export assets fields](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Download%20a%20file/10.%20Export%20assets%20fields.png?width=670&height=357&name=10.%20Export%20assets%20fields.png)

8. Click **'Export.' **Your asset files will be emailed to you and they will also appear under the "**Exports**" section of "**Products**" until it expires.

 

#### From the Product Overview page

You can download assets linked to products you select directly from the "All products" page. To do this: 
1. Select your products and click the **'Export' **button

![11. Export form product list](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Download%20a%20file/11.%20Export%20form%20product%20list.png?width=670&height=357&name=11.%20Export%20form%20product%20list.png)

2.   Here you will see four options for exporting: **CSV, XLSX, PDF, Product Data Sheet **and **Assets.**
Choose the CSV or XLSX button to receive the asset files as URLs (and also in a folder) or choose Assets to get them downloaded into a ZIP folder.

![12. Export from product list format](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Download%20a%20file/12.%20Export%20from%20product%20list%20format.png?width=670&height=357&name=12.%20Export%20from%20product%20list%20format.png)

3.    Change the file name. If downloading as a CSV or XLSX you can choose whether or not you want to download the assets. 4.    Click **'Export'**

![13. Export from product list format confirmation](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Download%20a%20file/13.%20Export%20from%20product%20list%20format%20confirmation.png?width=670&height=357&name=13.%20Export%20from%20product%20list%20format%20confirmation.png)

Your assets will be emailed to you and they will also appear under the "**Exports**" section of "**Products**" until it expires.
