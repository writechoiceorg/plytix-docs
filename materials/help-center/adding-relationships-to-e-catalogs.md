# Adding Relationships to Brand Portals

## How to add and display product relationships in Brand Portals

[Product relationships](https://help.plytix.com/relationships/create) are used for things like recommending similar products, creating sets and packs, upselling, and cross-selling. You can display these relationships in your [Brand Portals](https://help.plytix.com/en/create-and-manage-e-catalogs) for visitors to see. In this article, we'll show you how to add relationships to your products in a Brand Portal. 

---

### Adding Relationships to Brand Portals

Visitors to your Brand Portal will see product relationships in the product details. That is the page you enter when they click into a specific product in your Brand Portal. Adding product relationships to your Brand Portal is a two part process.

 

First, you must add the relationship attributes you want to include  in the Brand Portal
1. Click on **'Brand Portals'** in the top navigation menu.
2. Click on the name of the brand portal you want to modify _(this will take you to the Brand Portal's detail page)._
3. In the **'Attributes'** tab, add the relationships attributes you want to include in your Brand Portal.

![Attributes-Tab](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Adding%20Relationships%20to%20Brand%20Portals/Attributes-Tab.jpg?width=688&height=345&name=Attributes-Tab.jpg)

4. Next, click on the "**Layout**" tab of your Brand Portal and scroll down to the "**Detail settings**" section.

Make sure the "**Autofill content**" option is toggled off. Then, click '**+ Add**' to add a new Relationship component. 

![Components](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Adding%20Relationships%20to%20Brand%20Portals/Components.jpg?width=688&height=498&name=Components.jpg)

5. Click on the pencil icon to edit the component. This will open a side panel with options to configure your preferences for the component you selected. Here, you can rename your Relationship component, specify whether it should be displayed as grid or list format and choose the type of relationship. You can also choose which attributes will be displayed as the title of your product relationship, the thumbnail, as well as add up to 4 additional attributes to display in the section. 

 

![Edit component](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Adding%20Relationships%20to%20Brand%20Portals/Edit%20component.jpg?width=688&height=323&name=Edit%20component.jpg)

Don't forget to Save your changes. The relationship will now appear with your products in your Brand Portal.

ℹ️ You must add a new component for every relationship attribute you wish to include. For example, if you want the relationships "Matching," and "Related to," you must add two relationship components to your product page.

💡 Drag and drop components to arrange how they are displayed in the product pages of your Brand Portal.
