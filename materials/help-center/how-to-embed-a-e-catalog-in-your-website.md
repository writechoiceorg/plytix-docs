# Embedding a Brand Portal

## How to embed a Brand Portal made in Plytix onto your website

One of the best ways to share your Brand Portal is to embed it on your website just like you would do with a YouTube video, using [**an iframe**](https://html.com/tags/iframe/). When you embed a Brand Portal on your website it becomes part of youR site and is listed **under your domain URL**. In this article you will learn how to add your Brand Portal made in Plytix to your website. 

[Embedding your Brand Portal](#embed)

---

### Embedding your Brand Portal:

1. Copy the example iframe below:
 
 _https://ecatalogs.plytix.com/5e29fdd6e69e66e29d077553_

| <iframe width="950px" height="900px" src="https://ecatalogs.plytix.com/5e29fdd6e69e66e29d077553" frameborder="0"></iframe> |
| --- |

 
2. Replace the URL above with your portal URL (this can be found in the Brand Portal's detail page).

![Copy url](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Embedding%20a%20Brand%20Portal/Copy%20url.jpg?width=655&height=177&name=Copy%20url.jpg)

3. Check that the height and width are appropriate and change as necessary. This will expand or reduce the size of the iframe. 

4. Paste it into the area on your website where you want this to appear.
