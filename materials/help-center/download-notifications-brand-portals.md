# Defining Export and Notification Settings for Brand Portals

## Set how visitors can download information from your Brand Portals and who should be notified of it

Users can download files directly from a Brand Portal File tab, but a Brand Portal can also enable the options to download product data in Data Sheet format (CSV/XLSX). In this article you will learn how to enable export options in your Brand Portal and how to set up your notification preferences when exports are done.

[Managing PDF Exports](#pdf)

### Managing Exports

 

In Brand Portals, you can enable the following download options for product data:

[Data Sheet Exports](#data-sheet)

[PDF Exports](#pdf)

#### Data Sheet Exports

Data Sheet exports allow your visitors to choose to download product information in a CSV or XLSX format. You can decide which attributes from your portal are downloadable in this format by clicking on the **settings** icon. 

 

To activate data sheet exports in your Brand Portal:

1. Head to your Brand Portal's detail page. Then, click on the **"Settings" tab**.

2. Toggle on the boolean to make the export option available in your Brand Portal.

3. You can configure the options of the exports by clicking on the settings icon

![Settings-DataSheet](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Defining%20Export%20and%20Notification%20Settings%20for%20Brand%20Portals/Settings-DataSheet.jpg?width=688&height=309&name=Settings-DataSheet.jpg)

**Options:**
- Select which attributes get exported in this format
- Choose the order in which the attributes will be displayed in the data sheet

To add attributes available for export in this format:
1. Click the '**+ Add attributes**'  to select which attributes you want to make available for download
2. Organize the column output order by dragging and dropping the attributes

![Data-sheet-settings](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Defining%20Export%20and%20Notification%20Settings%20for%20Brand%20Portals/Data-sheet-settings.jpg?width=688&height=329&name=Data-sheet-settings.jpg)

ℹ️ The "**Exportable attributes**" section reflects the number of attributes you have added to your Data sheet export option, out of the total number of attributes in your Brand Portal. Click here to learn [how to add attributes to your Brand Portal](https://help.plytix.com/en/applying-attributes-to-e-catalogs). 

 

#### PDF Exports

To enable PDF exports in your Brand Portal, scroll to the section called "PDF export". Here, you can choose to enable PDF downloads of **specification sheets** or **price lists**. 

To configure your preferences for **specification sheets**:

1. First, toggle on the option to allow for PDF downloads of specification sheets

2. Click on the settings icon to configure your preferences

3. A side panel will open with two tabs: "**PDF Options**" and "**Branding Options**". 

![PDF export](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Defining%20Export%20and%20Notification%20Settings%20for%20Brand%20Portals/PDF%20export.jpg?width=688&height=253&name=PDF%20export.jpg)

Under "**PDF Options"**, you can choose your file name, you preferred orientation and layout. 

Then, define the attributes that will be used as the Product Title, Product Reference, and Main Image in your sheet.

You can also add as many additional attributes as you would like by clicking '+ Add attributes'. Drag and drop the attributes to choose the order in which they will be listed in your specification sheet.

![pdf settings (1)](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Defining%20Export%20and%20Notification%20Settings%20for%20Brand%20Portals/pdf%20settings%20(1).jpg?width=688&height=341&name=pdf%20settings%20(1).jpg)

 4. Under **"Branding Options"**, you can upload a file to be used as your company logo on the specification sheet. You also have the option to enter company information in text format; this will be located on the upper right-hand side of your sheet. 

Click 'Save' to save your preferences. 

![branding](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Defining%20Export%20and%20Notification%20Settings%20for%20Brand%20Portals/branding.jpg?width=688&height=284&name=branding.jpg)

 

To configure your preferences for **price lists**:

1. First, toggle on the option to allow for PDF downloads of price lists

2. Click on the settings icon to configure your preferences

3. A side panel will open with two tabs: "PDF Options" and "Branding Options". 

Under "**PDF Options**", you can choose your file name (which is the name of the downloaded file) and Document Title (title displayed on the PDF document itself).

You can also choose your preferred orientation for the document.

ℹ️ Note that the maximum number of columns that can be displayed varies according to the orientation you choose; **portrait** allows for up to 6 columns while **landscape** allows for up to 9 columns. 

![Price-list-1](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Defining%20Export%20and%20Notification%20Settings%20for%20Brand%20Portals/Price-list-1.jpg?width=688&height=342&name=Price-list-1.jpg)

4. Under **"Branding Options"**, you can upload your logo and enter your company information which will be displayed on your price list. 

Click 'Save' to save your preferences. 

![price-list-2-Branding](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Defining%20Export%20and%20Notification%20Settings%20for%20Brand%20Portals/price-list-2-Branding.jpg?width=688&height=308&name=price-list-2-Branding.jpg)

### Managing Account Download Notifications

You have the option to set up direct email notifications whenever a data sheet or PDF export is made in your Brand Portal. This notification will include the users' e-mail and a copy of the exported data sheet or PDF. 

As an account user, you can change the settings to whom the notifications get sent.

To edit notification settings for the data sheet and PDF exports in your Brand Portal:

1. Head over to the "Settings" tab of your Brand Portal's detail page.

2. Toggle on the option of "Notifications"

![Enabledisable email notification for exports (1)](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Defining%20Export%20and%20Notification%20Settings%20for%20Brand%20Portals/Enabledisable%20email%20notification%20for%20exports%20(1).jpg?width=688&height=356&name=Enabledisable%20email%20notification%20for%20exports%20(1).jpg)

3. Once you enable email notifications for exports, you will see that the owner's email address is automatically added by default and it cannot be removed from this list. Here, you can view the email addresses you've added for notifications and add new ones as needed.

![Owner email](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Defining%20Export%20and%20Notification%20Settings%20for%20Brand%20Portals/Owner%20email.jpg?width=688&height=250&name=Owner%20email.jpg)

4. After clicking **'+ Add email',** a modal will be displayed, and the first (default) option will be Internal (existing users). The internal option won't be available if there aren't more available emails. 

![Add-email-Internal](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Defining%20Export%20and%20Notification%20Settings%20for%20Brand%20Portals/Add-email-Internal.jpg?width=688&height=453&name=Add-email-Internal.jpg)

If you are adding an email of someone who is not a user in your account, click "**Guest**". Then, type out their email and check the box below to confirm. Click '**Add email**' to add the new email. 

![Guest](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Defining%20Export%20and%20Notification%20Settings%20for%20Brand%20Portals/Guest.jpg?width=688&height=443&name=Guest.jpg)

Emails that belong to users in your account will display their user role in the "Role" column, while emails that don't belong to your account will be listed as "Guest". You can check or uncheck the box under "Data sheet" and "PDF" export to enable/disable email notifications for each individual email address you have added. 

![Emails notifications](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Defining%20Export%20and%20Notification%20Settings%20for%20Brand%20Portals/Emails%20notifications.jpg?width=688&height=325&name=Emails%20notifications.jpg)

5. Click the 'x' to remove an email address from your Brand Portal notification settings, or uncheck the box to momentarily disable notifications for that particular email. After you've made your changes, click 'Save changes'.

![settings-new-emails](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Defining%20Export%20and%20Notification%20Settings%20for%20Brand%20Portals/settings-new-emails.jpg?width=688&height=389&name=settings-new-emails.jpg)
