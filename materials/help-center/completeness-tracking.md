# Working with Completeness Attributes

## How to create and manage completeness attributes in Plytix to easily track and monitor your product's status

Completeness attributes are great to keep track of a product's readiness for different purposes - e.g. tracking whether a product is ready to move from "draft" to "published," whether it has all the media and information needed to be shared on your website, or if it contains all the required fields for Shopify, to cite just a few examples. A completeness attribute is made up of other attributes and as you fill in information for those attributes, the completeness percentage grows. You can create as many Completeness Attributes as you'd like to help track your products.

### Creating a Completeness Attribute

To create a Completeness Attribute follow these steps: 
1. Navigate to **'Settings'** in the top menu.
2. The Settings area will automatically open in the "**Attributes**" section. If not, you may not have permissions to manage attributes.
3. Click the **'+ Create attribute'** button in the top left corner.
4. Choose "**Completeness Attribute**". 
5. Name your attribute. A label will be automatically generated (_you can change the name of your attribute at any time, but you are unable to change the label). _Click **'Next'**.

![Completess attribute](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Getting%20Started/Working%20with%20Completeness%20Attributes/New%20design%202026/Completess%20attribute.jpg?width=644&height=327&name=Completess%20attribute.jpg)

6. Under "Available attributes" select the attributes you want to define your completion. These will be shown on the right under "Selected attributes". 
7. Click **'Create Attribute'**. 

![add attributes ](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Getting%20Started/Working%20with%20Completeness%20Attributes/add%20attributes%20.jpg?width=670&height=254&name=add%20attributes%20.jpg)

💡  We suggest naming this attribute referencing the outcome of the completion (_Amazon_, _Web Ready_, _Spring Catalog_, etc).

### Viewing Completeness Attributes

To view a Completeness Attribute you can: 

1. Add it to your [product table view](https://help.plytix.com/table-views)

![PO - Completeness](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Getting%20Started/Working%20with%20Completeness%20Attributes/New%20design%202026/PO%20-%20Completeness.jpg?width=670&height=193&name=PO%20-%20Completeness.jpg)

2. See it in the** [Product Detail page](https://help.plytix.com/product-editing-overview).  **

To view which attributes are missing in a completeness attribute: 
1. Go to the Product Detail Page of your product
2. On the "Attribute Group"** **menu on the top, choose **'All custom attributes' **or any attribute group that contains a completeness attribute.
3. On the right side of the page you will see the completeness attributes listed. Click on one to expand the attributes that make up the completeness and see which have missing information. 

![Product Detail Page - Completeness (3)](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Getting%20Started/Working%20with%20Completeness%20Attributes/New%20design%202026/Product%20Detail%20Page%20-%20Completeness%20(3).jpg?width=670&height=279&name=Product%20Detail%20Page%20-%20Completeness%20(3).jpg)

💡 Create [attribute groups](https://help.plytix.com/en/product-attribute-groups) with all essential attributes that make up a completeness attribute to quickly find and fill in missing information.

### Filtering your products using a Completeness Attribute

You can use the Completeness Attributes to filter through your products. This helps you to identify which products have all the information complete and which are missing information. 

**To do this:**

1. Navigate to the** **Product Overview page

2. On the left "Filters" menu select the Completeness Attribute you want to filter by. A second dropdown will appear below. 

3. Select the condition you'd like to filter by (e.g. _is not equal to 100_). 

💡 Make sure to add the completeness attribute you are using to filter in your **table view** to get an immediate overview. 

Once you have applied a completeness attribute filter, you can simply check which information is still missing for specific products by hovering over the completeness chart of a product and it will display which attributes are complete and which ones are still missing information. 

![PO - Completeness (1)](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Getting%20Started/Working%20with%20Completeness%20Attributes/New%20design%202026/PO%20-%20Completeness%20(1).jpg?width=670&height=332&name=PO%20-%20Completeness%20(1).jpg)

https://help.plytix.com/hubfs/Help%20center/Managing%20products/Completeness/filter-by-completeness.png

💡 Use these filters to create dynamic [product lists](https://help.plytix.com/create-and-manage-product-lists) for internal use as well as to ensure you only send/share products that reach 100% completeness.

Working with Completeness Attributes and Product Families

You may want to use completeness attributes to assess the product readiness within your product families.

If you are interested in working with completeness attributes in product families, please keep in mind that you can add a completeness attribute to an attribute group, but you can’t add it to a family. In this case, the best way to manage a completeness attribute to assess product readiness within a specific to a family is to create a [**personalized table view**](https://help.plytix.com/en/table-views) for each family that you wish to view the completeness for. 

To do that:

1. Select the family you wish to view in your Product Overview Page.

![Families](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Getting%20Started/Working%20with%20Completeness%20Attributes/New%20design%202026/Families.jpg?width=670&height=181&name=Families.jpg)

2. Click **"Edit columns"** and add the completeness attribute you want to view.

3.  Drag and drop it to view it in earlier columns to find it easily. Click **"Apply"**

![Edit columns (1)](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Getting%20Started/Working%20with%20Completeness%20Attributes/New%20design%202026/Edit%20columns%20(1).jpg?width=670&height=433&name=Edit%20columns%20(1).jpg)

4. You can now view the completeness attributes you selected.

After editing your columns, click** "Unsaved changes"** > **"Save as new view"** in the right corner of the page. 

![Save view](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Getting%20Started/Working%20with%20Completeness%20Attributes/New%20design%202026/Save%20view.jpg?width=670&height=208&name=Save%20view.jpg)

5. Give your new view a name that will easily be recognizable for the purpose of the view such as "(Family Name) (Completeness)". Configure also the View settings, such as columns, filters, sorting, product family, or levels displayed. 

![Save view-1](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Getting%20Started/Working%20with%20Completeness%20Attributes/New%20design%202026/Save%20view-1.jpg?width=670&height=250&name=Save%20view-1.jpg)

You can choose to view any attributes you like by customizing your columns in your Product Overview Page, regardless of whether or not an attribute belongs to a specific family you select. However, this is different in your **product detail page**. If a product belongs to a family, and there are one or more attributes within a completeness attribute that do not belong to said family, then, the completeness attribute will not appear in that product's detail page  (even if it shows up on the table view of the product overview page).
