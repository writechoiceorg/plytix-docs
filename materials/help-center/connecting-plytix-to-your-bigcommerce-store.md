# Connecting Plytix to your BigCommerce Store

## How to connect your BigCommerce store and multiple storefronts to Plytix PIM

Creating and updating products in your BigCommerce store is easy with Plytix! With Plytix’s BigCommerce app, you can connect different storefronts in your BigCommerce account to your PIM with no coding required. In this article, you will learn how to access the Plytix app to connect your PIM with BigCommerce.

ℹ️ If you'd like to get access to Plytix's BigCommerce channel, please contact your Account Manager.

### Connecting to the Plytix App

The first step to connect your BigCommerce store to Plytix is to install the Plytix app.
 1. Access the **Apps** section on the sidebar menu of your BigCommerce account, then click **Marketplace**. Click the main button on the page to access the full list of BigCommerce apps.

![bigcommerce-apps](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Connecting%20Plytix%20to%20your%20BigCommerce%20Store/bigcommerce-apps.jpg?width=670&height=361&name=bigcommerce-apps.jpg)

 2. Search for **Plytix** and open the Plytix PIM app. This redirects you to Plytix's app listing, where you'll find all the information you need, including the access requirements to use BigCommerce with Plytix. Click **Install**.

![install-plytix-apps](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Connecting%20Plytix%20to%20your%20BigCommerce%20Store/install-plytix-apps.jpg?width=670&height=358&name=install-plytix-apps.jpg)

3. A disclaimer message pops up listing the type of access required by the Plytix app. These access rights are needed to create and update products from Plytix to BigCommerce. 4. Click **Confirm**.

![confirm-plytix-app](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Connecting%20Plytix%20to%20your%20BigCommerce%20Store/confirm-plytix-app.jpg?width=670&height=316&name=confirm-plytix-app.jpg)

5. Another popup appears with a link to be redirected to the Plytix platform. Click **Go to Plytix**.

 

![go-to-plytix](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Connecting%20Plytix%20to%20your%20BigCommerce%20Store/go-to-plytix.jpg?width=670&height=531&name=go-to-plytix.jpg)

6. You'll then find a window with log in or sign up options.

There are two ways to connect BigCommerce to Plytix:
- [Logging in to your Plytix account](#log-in)
- [Signing up to Plytix with a new account](#sign-up)

![bc-plytix-connector-welcome](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Connecting%20Plytix%20to%20your%20BigCommerce%20Store/bc-plytix-connector-welcome.jpg?width=670&height=695&name=bc-plytix-connector-welcome.jpg)

### Logging in to your Plytix account

If you already have a Plytix account, click **Log in**. After logging in, a dropdown of all the accounts you're a user in shows up.

You'll only be able to select an account to connect BigCommerce to if:
- The **channels module** is activated in the account, and
- You have **channel editing permissions**.

The **Connect the account** button automatically enables once an account meets both of these requirements.

ℹ️ If you're facing any issues, or would like to add channels in an account to access the BigCommerce connector, reach out to us via the chat icon in the bottom right of the login/sign-up page.

 1. Select an account and click **Connect this account**.

![select-plytix-account](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Connecting%20Plytix%20to%20your%20BigCommerce%20Store/select-plytix-account.jpg?width=580&height=626&name=select-plytix-account.jpg)

2. A window will pop up asking you to name your channel.

3. Choose a **storefront** to connect to the channel.

![set-up-bc-channel](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Connecting%20Plytix%20to%20your%20BigCommerce%20Store/set-up-bc-channel.jpg?width=670&height=375&name=set-up-bc-channel.jpg)

ℹ️ Only **one store** is allowed per Plytix account, but you can add as many storefronts belonging to that store as needed. BigCommerce itself refers to storefronts as "channels" in its own interface, so a BigCommerce store with several of its own "channels" translates to several storefronts you can connect, one Plytix channel per storefront. After setting up your BigCommerce connector, you can create a different BigCommerce channel and select a different storefront within the same store. See [Connecting Different Storefronts](#connect-storefronts) below.

### Signing up to Plytix with a New Account

If you don't have a Plytix account, you can sign up through Google Single Sign-On or by manually entering your email.

1. Sign up with your email or Google account.

![sign-up-plytix](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Connecting%20Plytix%20to%20your%20BigCommerce%20Store/sign-up-plytix.jpg?width=670&height=596&name=sign-up-plytix.jpg)

 

2. Verify your email address via the link sent to your inbox.

⚠️ The verification link expires in 24 hours, but you can access the page as many times as needed within this timeframe.

3. Set up your password, then click **Save and continue**.

ℹ️ If you're signing up through Google, the password step is skipped.

![setup-password](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Connecting%20Plytix%20to%20your%20BigCommerce%20Store/setup-password.jpg?width=670&height=393&name=setup-password.jpg)

 4. Set up your profile information, then click **Save profile information**.

![setup-profile](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Connecting%20Plytix%20to%20your%20BigCommerce%20Store/setup-profile.jpg?width=670&height=389&name=setup-profile.jpg)

5. Fill in your account information with your company's details, then click **Save the account and get started.**

![setup-account](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Connecting%20Plytix%20to%20your%20BigCommerce%20Store/setup-account.jpg?width=670&height=383&name=setup-account.jpg)

6. [Set up your BigCommerce store information](#bigcommerce-channel).

### Connecting Different Storefronts

After connecting your BigCommerce store to Plytix, you can also [create different channels](https://help.plytix.com/en/creating-a-channel) for the different storefronts within that same store.

To connect different BigCommerce storefronts to Plytix:
1. Click **Add channel** on the channels overview page.
2. Name your channel and select the **BigCommerce** channel type.
3. Select your **store** and choose your **storefront**.
4. Click **Add channel** to save.

Now you can start sending your products over from Plytix to the storefront you chose.
