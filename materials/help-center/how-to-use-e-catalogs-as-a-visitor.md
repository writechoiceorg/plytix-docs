# Navigating Brand Portals

## How to navigate and find information within a Brand Portal

If a brand or manufacturer has shared their Brand Portal with you, then they want to make sure you have access to product specifications and content whenever you may need them. This article will walk you through the different parts of a Brand Portal for a seamless interaction.

 

There are 4 main sections when you first open the portal: 

[Header and Navigation](#header)
[Filters](#filters)
[Viewing Products and Files](#viewing)
- [Product Overview and Selection](#product)
- [File Overview and Selection](#file)

[Download Selection](#download)

### 1. Header and Navigation

Here you can browse the portal and change the view. 

![Header and Navigation](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Navigating%20Brand%20Portals/Header%20and%20Navigation.jpg?width=688&height=129&name=Header%20and%20Navigation.jpg)

From left to right:
- **Filters** - Hide the filters from the overview to allow more products to be visible or to add filters to narrow your search.
- **Sort by** - Change the order of the products shown in the view
- **Tabs** - Switch between the "Products" view and the "Files" view
- **Search bar** - Look for a specific item

### 3. Viewing Products and Files

Below the tab-bar you will see options to view the Products or Files in a grid or list view.

![grid view](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Navigating%20Brand%20Portals/grid%20view.jpg?width=688&height=343&name=grid%20view.jpg)

![list-view-1](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Navigating%20Brand%20Portals/list-view-1.jpg?width=688&height=336&name=list-view-1.jpg)

#### a. Product Overview and Selection

Here you can browse products and select the items you want to add to your download. The Brand Portal sort of works like a content store, where you can browse products and add them to your cart. This makes it easy to customize the content you download, so that you don't need to download all the content every time you want only a few items worth of data.

![Product overview](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Navigating%20Brand%20Portals/Product%20overview.jpg?width=688&height=313&name=Product%20overview.jpg)

You can also **open each product by clicking on its card** to see a detailed product page for more information about each item. 

![Navigating Brand Portals](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Navigating%20Brand%20Portals/Navigating%20Brand%20Portals.jpg?width=688&height=432&name=Navigating%20Brand%20Portals.jpg)

#### b. File Overview and Selection

Some Brand Portals have a "Files" section enabled, where you can browse all the available images, documents, and other files the creator of the portal has provided. 

To get here: 
1. Go to the header and navigation area
2. Find the tabs 
3. Click **"Files"**

![Files1](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Navigating%20Brand%20Portals/Files1.jpg?width=688&height=305&name=Files1.jpg)

This section works the same as the Product Overview (selection and filtering), but with two main differences:
- You cannot click into individual files 
- In addition to selecting multiple files for download, you can download individual files directly from the files overview page, using the **"direct download"** icon on the corner of each file card

ℹ️ Products and Files have their own **distinct download selections**. However, anything you add to your download selection will be maintained when switching between products and files.
