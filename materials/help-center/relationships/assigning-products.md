# Assigning Products to a Relationship

## How to assign product relationships to different SKUs

After [creating your relationships](https://help.plytix.com/relationships/create), it's time to put them into use.

 

You can assign relationships:

[From a Product Detail's Page](#product)

[In Bulk from the Product Overview Page](#overview)

 

ℹ️ You can also assign relationships upon import. To see how to do this check out the [Import Relationships](https://help.plytix.com/relationships/create#import) section of the "Creating Relationships" article. 

 

---

### **Assigning Relationships from a Product Detail's Page**

You can enter an individual product from the Product Overview by clicking on a its SKU. This will take you to the Product Details Page. 

 

From a Product Detail's page:
1. Go to the '**Relationships'** tab.
2. Click '**Add related products.'**

![product-relationships-add](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Assign%20products%20to%20a%20relatioship/product-relationships-add.png?width=640&height=342&name=product-relationships-add.png)

3. Select which relationship you want to assign.
**

![product-relationsips-add-type](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Assign%20products%20to%20a%20relatioship/product-relationsips-add-type.png?width=644&height=342&name=product-relationsips-add-type.png)

**
4. Select the products with which you want to create a relationship. If you want to create a relationship that works both ways, check the **'Relate products back'** box. Then click **'Add products.'**

![product-relationsips-add-products](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Assign%20products%20to%20a%20relatioship/product-relationsips-add-products.png?width=639&height=340&name=product-relationsips-add-products.png)

5. You will now see the products related to your selected product
**

![product-relationships](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Assign%20products%20to%20a%20relatioship/product-relationships.png?width=633&height=337&name=product-relationships.png)

**

 💡 Note that product relationships are only applied to the product you select unless you check the '**Relate products back**' box. This means that in the example above, checking the box will make the same relationship appear on both the blue bowl and the red, steel, and glass bowls. If the box was **not **checked, the relationship would only apply _from_ the blue bowl _to_ the other bowls selected, but not the inverse.

### **Assigning Relationships from the Product Overview Page**

You can manage relationships to products directly from the Product Overview in the "**All products**" section of the PIM. You can do this for one or many products.

 

From the Product Overview: 
1. Select the products whose relationship you want to manage.
2. Click '**Edit products'.** 

![overview-relationships-edit](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Assign%20products%20to%20a%20relatioship/overview-relationships-edit.png?width=630&height=336&name=overview-relationships-edit.png)

3. Select '**Edit relationships'** 
4. Select the action you want to accomplish (**'add'/'remove'**).
5. Select the **'[Quantity](https://help.plytix.com/en/relationships/create)'** (only available if _adding_ a relationship).
6. Select the product you want to relate.
7. Click '**Save.'**

[![overview-relationships-edit-02](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Assign%20products%20to%20a%20relatioship/overview-relationships-edit-02.png?width=670&height=358&name=overview-relationships-edit-02.png)](https://help.plytix.com/hubfs/Help%20center/Managing%20products/Bulk%20edit%20products/bulk-edit-relationships.png)

ℹ️ When managing relationships from the Product Overview, you can add up to 300 products as a relationship.
