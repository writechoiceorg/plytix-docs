# Changing Your Plan

## How to upgrade or downgrade your Plytix account to meet your needs

At Plytix we have [different plans](https://www.plytix.com/pricing/) suitable for your needs. In addition to these, you can mix and match the features you need to create your own custom plan by adding different add-ons or credits to your subscription. The management of your plan is done in the **'Plan'** tab of the account settings area.

ℹ️ Your plan type will appear in** **the top-right corner of your account settings.

---

### Accessing the accounts area

To access the accounts area in Plytix, you can navigate to [accounts.plytix.com.](https://accounts.plytix.com/)

You can also access this area by clicking on your profile picture in the bottom-left corner of the platform. From here, click on the settings icon and select the **'Plan'** tab.

### Information about your plan

Here you can find the next payment date and amount for your current plan. If you would like to upgrade, click on **'Upgrade now' **button in the top-right corner. You may also reach out to your account manager directly or connect with us by emailing help@plytix.com if you need assistance with upgrading or downgrading your plan.
