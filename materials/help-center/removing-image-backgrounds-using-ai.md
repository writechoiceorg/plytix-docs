# Editing Images Using AI

## How to use AI in Plytix to replace backgrounds, relight assets, create custom prompts, upscale images, and get them Shopify-ready

In Plytix, transforming your images is quick and simple. With AI Edit, you can automatically replace image backgrounds with a solid color, relight images, create custom prompts, upscale resolution, and optimise images for Shopify, all without leaving the platform.

You can apply this to a single image or process multiple images at once, so your product catalog stays consistent and channel-ready.

### Replacing Image Backgrounds

Replace an image's background entirely with a clean, solid color, ideal for creating a consistent, uniform look across your product catalog. The product itself stays untouched: its shape, color, texture, and any logos or branding are preserved exactly as they appear in the original image.

1. Select an image from the **All assets** page. (You can also edit the image directly from its asset detail page.)

2. Click the **AI Edit** (🪄) icon.

3. In the modal, choose **Replace Background**.

4. A panel will appear with the following options:
- **Background Color**: choose a color using the color picker, or enter a hex code directly. In the color picker, click **Apply** to confirm your color, or **Cancel** to back out.
- **Add shadow** checkbox: tick this to add a soft shadow beneath the product, consistent with a studio lighting setup. Leave it unticked to keep the background completely flat, with no shadow.
- **Resolution**: choose **2x** or **4x** to increase the pixel dimensions of the output image.
- **Size (px)**: set the exact **Width** and **Height** of the output image.
- **Format**: choose **WEBP**, **JPG**, or **PNG**.

5. Click the **Preview** button to see the result before generating.

6. Click the **Generate** button to apply the change and create the new asset.

💡 **Resolution** increases the pixel dimensions of the output image, but because generative AI is used to fill in the extra space, it can't guarantee a sharper or higher-quality result, the AI may reinterpret fine details rather than simply enlarging them precisely.

ℹ️ This action consumes credits.

This will create a copy of your original asset with the applied edits. The original image is not overwritten and remains unchanged.

### **
**Relighting Your Images

Relighting your images with AI in Plytix helps you quickly improve and adjust the lighting of your product photos in just a few clicks. This makes it easy to create more consistent visuals across your catalog, especially when images come from different environments or setups.

**To relight a single image:**

1. Select an image from the **All assets** page. (You can also edit the image directly from its asset detail page.)

2. Click the **AI Edit** (🪄) icon.

3. In the modal, choose **Relight**. 4. A panel will appear. On the left side, you will see the configuration options, including Relight styles and editable parameters. On the right side, you will see the preview of the result.

![Relight](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Removing%20Image%20Backgrounds%20Using%20AI/New%20Design%202026/Relight.jpg?width=670&height=446&name=Relight.jpg)

5. Click the **Preview** button to test your settings.

6. Click the **Generate** button to apply the changes and create the new asset.

ℹ️ This action consumes credits. 

This will create a copy of your original asset with the applied edits. The original image is not overwritten and remains unchanged.

### **
**Creating a Custom Prompt 

With AI Edit in Plytix, you can create your own custom prompts to define how your images should be transformed. You simply describe the result you want, and the Plytix AI applies it to your asset. This gives you more flexibility to adjust and enhance product images so they better match your needs. 

**To create a custom prompt for a single image:**

1. Select an image from the **All assets** page. (You can also edit the image directly from its asset detail page.)

2. Click the **AI Edit** (🪄) icon.

3. In the modal, choose **Custom prompt**.

**

![Custom AI](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Removing%20Image%20Backgrounds%20Using%20AI/New%20Design%202026/Custom%20AI.jpg?width=670&height=322&name=Custom%20AI.jpg)

**

 4. In the text field, describe the transformation you want applied to the image, or click **Saved prompts** to load a prompt you've used before ([check out Saved prompts below](#saved-prompts)).

![assets-custom_prompt-save_prompts](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Editing%20Images%20Using%20AI/assets-custom_prompt-save_prompts.png?width=670&height=383&name=assets-custom_prompt-save_prompts.png)

5. Click **Preview** to see the result before generating, then click **Generate** to apply.

![assets-custom_prompt](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Editing%20Images%20Using%20AI/assets-custom_prompt.png?width=670&height=383&name=assets-custom_prompt.png)

**Not sure what to write?** Custom prompts work best when you're specific about the result you want. Here are some examples to get you started:
- _"Place the product on a white marble surface with soft shadows"_
- _"Add a warm sunset background"_
- _"Keep the background but remove the mannequin"_

ℹ️ This action consumes credits. This will create a copy of your original asset with the applied edits. The original image is not overwritten and remains unchanged.

#### Saved prompts

If you write a prompt that works well, you can save it to reuse later rather than writing it from scratch each time. Saved prompts are especially useful when you're applying the same transformation across multiple assets. For example, placing all your products on the same background or applying a consistent style across a catalogue shoot.

To save a prompt, write it in the text field and click **Save prompt** before generating. To use a saved prompt, click the **Saved prompts** button above the text field and select the one you want, it will load into the field, where you can edit it further if needed before generating.

⚠️ Users can save up to 10 prompts per account.

### Upscaling Your Images

AI Upscale increases the resolution of your images without losing visual quality. This is useful when your original assets are too low-resolution for certain channels or print requirements.

**To upscale a single image:**
1. Select an image from the **All assets** page. (You can also edit the image directly from its asset detail page.)
2. Click the **AI Edit** (🪄) icon.
3. In the modal, choose **Upscale**.
4. Configure your upscale settings:
 - **Image size**: Select a ratio from the dropdown (e.g. 1:1, 2:3, 3:2, 3:4, 4:3). The pixel dimensions will fill automatically based on your selection, but you can adjust them manually.
 - **Scale factor**: Choose between 2x, 4x or 8x upscaling.
 - **Added Detail**: Controls how much creative freedom the AI has when generating texture, edges and fine visual information at the new resolution.
 - **Minimal**: stays close to the original, adding only what's needed for a clean upscale.
 - **Balanced**: adds more texture and sharpness while staying faithful to the original.
 - **Maximum**: most creative freedom, sharper results but further from the original.

5. Click **Generate** to create the upscaled asset.

![assets-upscaler](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Editing%20Images%20Using%20AI/assets-upscaler.png?width=670&height=399&name=assets-upscaler.png)

💡 **Scale factor** determines how much larger the output image will be compared to the original, for example, choosing 4x on a 500×500 image produces a 2000×2000 result. Because AI Upscale uses generative AI to fill in the extra space rather than simply stretching the original pixels, results are often sharper than a basic resize, though the AI may reinterpret some fine details along the way rather than enlarging them precisely. Use the **Added Detail** setting above to control how much creative freedom the AI takes in that process.

💡 Use **Minimal/Balanced** for accuracy (e.g. product photography), **Maximum** for visual impact over fidelity.

ℹ️ This action consumes credits.

This will create a copy of your original asset with the applied edits. The original image is not overwritten and remains unchanged.

### Shopify Ready

Get your images ready for Shopify. Shopify Ready automatically optimises your images to meet Shopify's recommended image standards, so your assets are listing-ready without any manual resizing or converting. Choose a product listing, banner, or collection preset to apply recommended Shopify image settings, then adjust if needed.

**To use Shopify Ready on a single image:**
1. Select an image from the **All assets** page. (You can also edit the image directly from its asset detail page.)
2. Click the **AI Edit** (🪄) icon.
3. In the modal, choose **Shopify Ready**.
4. Select a preset from the dropdown:
 - **Shopify listing** (2048×2048)
 - **Shopify banner** (1920×1080)
 - **Shopify collection** (1024×1024)

5. Adjust the ratio, pixel dimensions, format, and max file size if needed.
6. Click the **Preview** button to check the result.
7. Click the **Generate** button to create the optimised asset.

![assets-shopificator](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Editing%20Images%20Using%20AI/assets-shopificator.png?width=670&height=399&name=assets-shopificator.png)

ℹ️ This action consumes credits.

This will create a copy of your original asset with the applied edits. The original image is not overwritten and remains unchanged.

### **Editing Images in Bulk**

To edit multiple images at once using AI:
1. Head to the **All Assets** page.
2. Choose the images you want to edit.
3. In the bulk actions menu, click **AI Edit**, and select one of the editing options.

![Bulk action (1)](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Removing%20Image%20Backgrounds%20Using%20AI/New%20Design%202026/Bulk%20action%20%20(1).jpg?width=670&height=338&name=Bulk%20action%20%20(1).jpg)

Follow the same steps to generate your transformed images and save them to your library.

### **
Managing AI Image Jobs **

Depending on how many images you’re transforming at once, your AI progress may continue running in the background after you’ve saved your edits. 

You can continue working in Plytix while your new images are processed. In the meantime, you can:
- **Track progress**: You can monitor active jobs in the **Process Queue** section through the **Activity Log** button.
- **Cancel jobs**: To stop an AI bulk image transformation that is currently running, click the **Cancel** button in the **Process Queue**.

![Process queue](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Removing%20Image%20Backgrounds%20Using%20AI/New%20Design%202026/Process%20queue.jpg?width=670&height=284&name=Process%20queue.jpg)

-

**Review results: **Once the process is complete, you’ll get an in-tool notification. Check the newly generated assets to make sure they meet your quality standards.

### **
Frequently Asked Questions**

**Will AI overwrite my original images?**

No, AI edits will not overwrite your images.  AI-generated image transformations are saved as new assets. Original images remain unchanged. 

**What happens if some images fail to process?**

Failures may occur due to low image quality or unsupported formats. You can retry the transformation or contact our support team at **help@plytix.com**

**Can I apply AI edits to any image?**

Yes, you can edit images using AI for all the image formats supported in Plytix. Check out all our  [supported file types](https://help.plytix.com/en/supported-file-types). Please note that only image files can be edited with AI in Plytix. Currently, remove background only supports standard product images.
