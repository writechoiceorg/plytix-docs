# MULTIPLY Operation

## How to use the MULTIPLY operation in formulas

[Definition](#definition)

[Example](#example)

[Syntax Guide](#syntax)

[Formula In Use](#formula)

 

---

### Definition

The MULTIPLY operation returns the product of two or more numbers.

 

---

### Example

MULTIPLY(1, 2, 3, 4)

Result:
24

 

---

### Syntax Guide

MULTIPLY(value1,value2,[value3…])

**value1** - The first multiplicand

**value2** - The second multiplicand

**[value3..].** - [OPTIONAL] additional values to multiply

 

---

### Formula In Use

MULTIPLY($ATT.PRICE,0.8)

In this example, we are applying a discount of 20% to the store price attribute.
