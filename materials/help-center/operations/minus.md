### Definition

The MINUS operation allows you to subtract one number from another.

---

### Example

MINUS(15,5)

Result:
10

---

### Syntax Guide

MINUS(**value1**, **value2**)

**value1** - The number to be subtracted from

**value2** - The number to subtract from value1
