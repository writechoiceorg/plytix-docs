### Definition

The SUM operation allows you to add two or more numbers together.

---

### Example

SUM(1,2,11)

Result:
14

---

### Syntax Guide

SUM(value1, [value2, ...])

**value1** - The first number or range to add together

**value2, ...** - [ OPTIONAL ] - Additional numbers or ranges to add to value1
