# Linking and Unlinking Assets with Products Manually

## How to assign assets to products individually or in bulk

One of the great things about using Plytix is that you can link your products and your assets so that they are easy to use and find. In Plytix, you only need to upload your file once, and then you can use it in multiple places. In this article, we will explain the different ways to link or unlink an asset to a product so that they can be exported or used together.

### Asset Overview

To manually link asset(s) from the Assets overview screen:

💡 This article explains the manual linking process. If you want to link assets to products automatically based on asset file name, [click here to learn about **Autolinking Assets with Products in Bulk**](https://help.plytix.com/en/linking-assets-with-products-in-bulk#autolinking)**.**

ℹ️ You can link up to 1,000 assets at a time this way.
1. Select the asset(s) you want to link.
2. From the options that appear at the bottom of the table, click **Link products**. 

![assets-link-manual_selection](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/assets-link-manual_selection.png?width=644&height=343&name=assets-link-manual_selection.png)

3. Choose to link products manually, or based on asset file names (see [Autolinking](#autolinking) for that option). If you choose an attribute that can hold more than one asset, you'll have the option to **Add** the new assets to the gallery, or **Replace existing** ones in it.

![assets-link-manual_selection-2](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/assets-link-manual_selection-2.png?width=644&height=343&name=assets-link-manual_selection-2.png)

4. Select the media attribute where you want the assets to appear, then select the products you want to link them to

![assets-link-manual_selection-3](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/assets-link-manual_selection-3.png?width=644&height=343&name=assets-link-manual_selection-3.png)

5. Click **Save.**

Linked assets appear in the **Assets** tab of the product's Detail page, as well as within the specific attribute on its **Attributes** tab.

ℹ️ If you choose an attribute that can only store one asset, like **Thumbnail**, you won't see the **Add** and **Replace existing** options.

⚠️ You can link no more than 300 assets to a single product.

### Asset Detail Page

From the Asset Overview, click an asset to enter its Detail page. This takes you to the **Products** tab of the asset's Detail page.

**To link assets from the asset detail page:**
1. **Click Link Products.

![assets-detail-link](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/assets-detail-link.png?width=644&height=343&name=assets-detail-link.png)

**
2. In the popup, select the attribute you want to assign this asset to. If you choose an attribute that can store more than one asset, you'll have the option to **Add** the new asset, or **Replace existing**. Attributes that can only store one asset (like **Thumbnail**) won't show these options.

![assets-detail-link-2](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/assets-detail-link-2.png?width=644&height=343&name=assets-detail-link-2.png)

3. Choose the product(s) you want to link, then click **Next**. Narrow down the products shown by selecting a **Family** from the dropdown, if you'd like. Click **Save**.

![asset-detail-link-3](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/asset-detail-link-3.png?width=644&height=343&name=asset-detail-link-3.png)

The products you just linked now appear in the **Products** tab of the asset's Detail page.

![asset-detail-linked](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/asset-detail-linked.png?width=670&height=357&name=asset-detail-linked.png)

ℹ️ Linking an asset to a product automatically assigns it to the **Assets** overview tab and the system attribute **Assets**.

**To unlink an asset from a product:**
1. From the **Products** tab of the asset's Detail page, select the products you'd like to unlink.
2. Click **Unlink** in the options that appear above the table. Confirm by clicking **Unlink** again, and the product is removed from the asset's **Products** tab.

![asset-detail-unlink](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/asset-detail-unlink.png?width=670&height=357&name=asset-detail-unlink.png)

###
Product Overview

**To link an asset from the Product Overview screen:**
1. Select the product(s) you want to link assets to. From the options that appear at the bottom of the page, click **Edit media**.
2. Select **Add new assets **and choose the file(s) to upload, or select **Link existing assets** and choose which asset(s) to link.**

![overview-edit-edit_media-import](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/overview-edit-edit_media-import.png?width=644&height=343&name=overview-edit-edit_media-import.png)

 ** **

![overview-edit-edit_media-options-2](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/overview-edit-edit_media-options-2.png?width=644&height=343&name=overview-edit-edit_media-options-2.png)

**
3. Select the attribute you want to link the asset to. For Media Gallery attributes, choose **Add** or **Replace existing**. Click **Link**.
**

![overview-edit-edit_media-options](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/overview-edit-edit_media-options.png?width=644&height=343&name=overview-edit-edit_media-options.png)

**

Once linked, your assets appear on the Detail page of the selected products, under the **Assets** tab, an overview of every asset related to that product.

![product-detail-assets](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/product-detail-assets.png?width=670&height=357&name=product-detail-assets.png)

💡 You can view these assets in a list or in a grid.

**
To unlink assets: **
1. In the **Assets** tab of a product, select the assets you'd like to unlink.
2. Click **Unlink assets** below the table.

![product-detail-assets-unlink](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/product-detail-assets-unlink.png?width=670&height=357&name=product-detail-assets-unlink.png)

### **Attribute Tab of the Product Detail Page**

You can also link assets to media attributes directly within a product's Detail page. For example, adding an image to a product's **Thumbnail** attribute.

ℹ️ This requires [media-type attributes to already exist](https://help.plytix.com/managing-products/create-attributes-and-choose-attribute-types) in your account.

**To link assets as attributes: ** 1. Go to a product's Detail page

2. On the **Attributes** tab, find the media attribute you want to add assets to. Hover over it and click to edit.

![product-detail-media_attribute](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/product-detail-media_attribute.png?width=620&height=330&name=product-detail-media_attribute.png)

3. If changing the thumbnail, hover over the image to reveal the **Change thumbnail** icon.

![product-detail-thumbnail-add](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/product-detail-thumbnail-add.png?width=620&height=330&name=product-detail-thumbnail-add.png)

4. Click the plus icon to add more assets to the attribute. This opens the **Link assets** window

![product-detail-asset-add](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/product-detail-asset-add.png?width=620&height=330&name=product-detail-asset-add.png)

5. From here, you can either:
- **Select an existing asset**: browse or search by asset name, then check the box on each asset you want to add. Use **Select all** to select every asset shown at once.
- **Upload a new asset**: click the upload icon next to the **Only product's assets** toggle to open a drag-and-drop area, then drag in your file or click to browse for one.

![product-detail-asset-add-2](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/product-detail-asset-add-2.png?width=620&height=330&name=product-detail-asset-add-2.png)

 **

![product-detail-asset-add-3](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/product-detail-asset-add-3.png?width=670&height=357&name=product-detail-asset-add-3.png)

**  

💡 To link assets that aren't currently linked to this product, disable **Only product's assets**, letting you select any asset from your account.  

![product-detail-asset-add-4](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/product-detail-asset-add-4.png?width=670&height=357&name=product-detail-asset-add-4.png)

6. Once you've made your selection, click **Save**. 

7. You're redirected to the product Detail page. Click **Save changes** to save the assets you just linked.

![how to add info](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/how%20to%20add%20info.png?width=655&height=349&name=how%20to%20add%20info.png)

 

**To unlink an asset:**

Click the pencil icon on the media attribute to edit it, hover over an asset, and click the trash can icon to unlink it.

![product-detail-asset-unlink](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Linking%20and%20Unlinking%20Assets%20with%20Products%20Manually/product-detail-asset-unlink.png?width=670&height=357&name=product-detail-asset-unlink.png)
