# Configuring the Search Bar

## How to configure the product overview search bar to search for different attributes

The search bar in Product Overview lets you search through your products by **SKU** and **Label** by default. You can change this in the search bar settings to include up to **four** different attributes in your search. This article lists the attribute types available for use in the search bar, and teaches you how to change them in the search bar settings.

 

 

💡  Use [product filters](https://help.plytix.com/filtering) together with the search bar for a more granular search.

### Available Attribute Types
 The following are the attribute types you can search by:
- Short text
- Paragraph
- Dropdown
- Multi-select
- URL

---

### Changing the Search Bar Settings

To personalize your search bar:
1. Go to the navigation menu and click** "Products". **Then, click **"All Products"** from the dropdown. This takes you to **Product Overview**.
2. Hover over the search bar and click the three dots that appear to its right.
https://help.plytix.com/hubfs/Help%20center/Managing%20products/Filtering%20and%20search/access-search-bar-configuration.png

![1-searching](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Searching/1-searching.png?width=670&height=357&name=1-searching.png)

       3. Choose up to 4 attributes to look for 

![2-searching](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Searching/2-searching.png?width=670&height=357&name=2-searching.png)

        4. Click **"Apply" **to update your settings
