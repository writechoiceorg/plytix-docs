# Deleting Assets

## How to delete assets you no longer want in your account, individually or in bulk.

Deleting assets in Plytix is one way to manage your assets. If you don't wish to permanently delete an asset from Plytix, you may also [replace the asset](https://help.plytix.com/replace-a-file), [set it to private](https://help.plytix.com/file-privacy) or unlink it from [Products](https://help.plytix.com/link-and-unlink-files-to-a-product) or Brand Portals. However, if you've determined that deleting assets is the correct option, follow the steps below to delete a single asset or multiple assets at a time.

[Deleting an Individual Asset from its Detail Page](#individual_deletion_of_asset)

[Bulk Deleting Assets from the Assets Overview Page](#bulk_deletion_of_assets)

_*Skip to a section in this article by clicking on the links above_

_⚠️_ Deleting a file cannot be undone, so make sure this is what you want to do.

---

###
Deleting an Individual Asset from its Detail Page

Navigate to the Asset’s details screen. On the right side of the screen, select the trash can icon from the actions menu. 

![Delete- Assets 1](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Deleting%20Assets/new%20design%202026/Delete-%20Assets%201.jpg?width=670&height=211&name=Delete-%20Assets%201.jpg)

A panel will appear and you will be asked to type "DELETE" to confirm deletion. If you do not type "DELETE" the asset will not be deleted.

---

###
Bulk Deleting Assets from the Assets Overview Page

To delete an asset from the Assets overview screen, select the asset(s) you want to delete, then click the** trash can icon: **

![Bulk deleting](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Deleting%20Assets/new%20design%202026/Bulk%20deleting.jpg?width=670&height=375&name=Bulk%20deleting.jpg)

You can also quickly select a larger quantity of assets, by clicking the multi-select button. This gives you a selection of **all assets on the current page.

**If you want to select all assets in Plytix at once, click the button below to select all assets before pressing the **Delete** button.

![Delete all assets](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Deleting%20Assets/new%20design%202026/Delete%20all%20assets.jpg?width=670&height=359&name=Delete%20all%20assets.jpg)
