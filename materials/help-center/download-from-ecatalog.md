# Downloading Content from a Brand Portal

## How to download content from a Plytix Brand Portal in data sheet or PDF format

Brand Portals are product content portals where visitors can browse and download content directly from the page. In this article, you will learn how to download content from a Brand Portal.

[Downloading a Datasheet](#datasheet)

ℹ️ Please note that download options are **managed by the creators of the portals, **so they may not have all download options enabled. Therefore, options will vary based on the portal you visit.

---

### Download Formats

Brand Portals enable you to download different types of product content. They may have product specifications, images, videos, and other files available for you to download. Here are the different formats available for exporting information from a Brand Portal:
- Spreadsheet with product content and file URLs
- Specification Sheets and Price Lists in PDF format
- Individual files
- ZIP files with multiple items

💡 Before you get started downloading content, learn [how to navigate a Brand Portal](https://help.plytix.com/how-to-use-e-catalogs-as-a-visitor).

### Selecting Products and Files

#### Products

From the product overview page, use the** **switches on the top right corner of each product to make your selection. You can select each product individually or bulk select the whole page. 

You have the option to **'Review Selection' **or click on the download options to begin your download. 

![Download-Brand Portal1.2](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Downloading%20Content%20from%20a%20Brand%20Portal/Download-Brand%20Portal1.2.jpg?width=670&height=292&name=Download-Brand%20Portal1.2.jpg)

You can also select a product individually from a product detail's page.

![brand portal - download 2.1 ](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Downloading%20Content%20from%20a%20Brand%20Portal/brand%20portal%20-%20download%202.1%20.jpg?width=670&height=309&name=brand%20portal%20-%20download%202.1%20.jpg)

 

💡 Don't worry, any previous selections you have made will stick around. For example, once you have selected an individual product from a product's detail page, then navigate back to the product overview, the product will still be selected. 

#### Files

To select files, head to the "Files" tab and select each file individually using the switches on the top right corner of your file, or bulk select the whole page.

![Downloading Content from a Brand Portal](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Downloading%20Content%20from%20a%20Brand%20Portal/Downloading%20Content%20from%20a%20Brand%20Portal.jpg?width=670&height=238&name=Downloading%20Content%20from%20a%20Brand%20Portal.jpg)

 ℹ️ When downloading from the Files tab, your selected assets will automatically arrive as a single ZIP file with everything in one folder.

You can also download a file linked to a product from a product's detail page.

![File_download](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Downloading%20Content%20from%20a%20Brand%20Portal/File_download.jpg?width=670&height=360&name=File_download.jpg)

### Downloading a Datasheet

You can download product data including related files using the **Datasheet export option. **

![Download-Datasheet1](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Downloading%20Content%20from%20a%20Brand%20Portal/Download-Datasheet1.jpg?width=670&height=317&name=Download-Datasheet1.jpg)

This will open up further options for how to format your spreadsheet:
- Select your data format - CSV or XLSX.
- Select the product attributes you want to see in your spreadsheet.
- Choose to include a ZIP file of all selected product assets. You can also define the file types you want to download and if you would like to resize them. 

 ℹ️ If you choose to include a ZIP of assets, you'll receive a ZIP file containing the datasheet plus a separate folder for each selected product, with that product's related assets inside.

 

![Data sheet-download](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Downloading%20Content%20from%20a%20Brand%20Portal/Data%20sheet-download.jpg?width=670&height=377&name=Data%20sheet-download.jpg)

To finalize your download, provide an email address where the information will be sent, then click **'Download.' **Your content will be packaged up and sent to you via email with a link that will expire after 15 days.

ℹ️  The order in which your selected products are populated in your data sheet is dependent on the order you have chosen the products to be displayed using the "**Sorting**" option. [Set sorting conditions and define display order](https://help.plytix.com/en/how-to-use-e-catalogs-as-a-visitor) in the products tab to customize your output.

### Downloading a PDF

To download a selection of products in PDF, click the download icon on the upper right hand side of the page, and select "**Download PDF**" 

![Download pdf 2](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Downloading%20Content%20from%20a%20Brand%20Portal/Download%20pdf%202.jpg?width=670&height=314&name=Download%20pdf%202.jpg)

A popup window will appear for you to select which type of file you'd like to download.

You can choose to download all product in a single file selecting the "**One file**" option, or have a separate file for each individual product selecting the "**Multiple files**" option.

Provide an email address where the information will be sent, then click "**Download**".

![Specification sheet1.2.](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Downloading%20Content%20from%20a%20Brand%20Portal/Specification%20sheet1.2..jpg?width=670&height=389&name=Specification%20sheet1.2..jpg)

### Accessing Downloaded Content

When downloading from a Brand Portal, **you will receive your selection via email** through a download link that expires after 15 days. 

In the e-mail, you will see the name of the portal you downloaded from and a **'Download your files'** button.

![Email-download-your-files](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Downloading%20Content%20from%20a%20Brand%20Portal/Email-download-your-files.jpg?width=670&height=371&name=Email-download-your-files.jpg)

When clicking the **'Download your files'** button, you will be taken to a page where you can download your selection. 

⚠️ If the Brand Portal is password protected, you will need to input the password to receive your downloaded content
