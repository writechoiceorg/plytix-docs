# Keyboard Shortcuts

## A guide on how to navigate through Plytix using your keyboard commands

Navigating through your PIM is made even easier due to the reduced number of mouse clicks you can achieve 🖱️ by using keyboard shortcuts instead ⌨️ In this guide you can find the different shortcuts Plytix offers to help you seamlessly navigate your PIM.

ℹ️ The following shortcuts apply to both Windows and Mac. `CTRL` and `ALT` refer to Windows options, while  `CMD` and `OPT` apply to the Mac version.

💡 In your PIM, hover over the sections highlighted in this guide to find a hint of the keyboard commands that apply.

---

### **Searching products**

In the Product Overview page, press the following keys:

`CMD/CTRL + OPT/ALT + K`

The search bar will be highlighted so you can type in your search directly.

### **Showing/hiding filters**

To display or hide filters in the Product Overview page, press the following keys:

`CMD/CTRL + OPT/ALT + F`

### **Creating a new product**

To create a new product in the Product Overview page, press the following keys:
`CMD/CTRL + OPT/ALT + N`
  

This will automatically open the modal to create a new product.

### Undoing/redoing a change

To** undo** a change in the Product Overview, press the following keys:

`CMD/CTRL + Z`

This will cause the last change you made to a product attribute to be undone.

To **redo** a change in the Product Overview, press the following keys:

_Mac version_

`CMD + SHIFT + Z`

_Windows version_

`CTRL + SHIFT + Z```

or

`CTRL + Y`

ℹ️ You can undo/redo up to 10 changes at a time.

---

### Selecting multiple products in a row

To select multiple products in a row, hold the `SHIFT` key while selecting the products.
