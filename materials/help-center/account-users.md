# Inviting users and managing your team

## How to add, delete, and manage users in your Plytix account

This article covers everything you need to manage your team: inviting users, changing roles, removing users, and transferring account ownership.

You may skip to a specific section of this article by clicking on the links below.  

ℹ️  Only **Owners** and **Admins** have permissions to complete these actions.   [Accessing team settings](#accessing-team-settings)   [Inviting a new user](#inviting-new-user)    [Managing pending invitations](#pending-invitations)   [Changing a user's role](#changing-user-role)   [Deleting an existing user](#deleting-user)   [Transferring account ownership](#transferring-ownership) #what-next  

---

### **Accessing team settings**
 Navigate directly to [accounts.plytix.com](http://accounts.plytix.com) into the URL bar, or follow these steps within the platform:   1. Click on the account name in the bottom-left corner of the platform
2. Click on the settings icon

![Account_Settings_Overview](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Inviting%20users%20and%20managing%20your%20team/Account_Settings_Overview.png?width=670&height=357&name=Account_Settings_Overview.png)

   Once on the account settings page, navigate to the **"Team" **tab.  

---

### Inviting a new user
 There is no limit to how many users can be invited to an account.   To invite a new user:

1. In the **Team** tab, click the **+** button next to the active user count

![Account-team](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Inviting%20users%20and%20managing%20your%20team/Account-team.png?width=652&height=347&name=Account-team.png)

2. A **Send invitation** pop-up will appear. Enter the new user's email address, select a role (_we will go over how to change the user role in the next section)_
3. Click **Send invitation**
  

![Account-send_invitation](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Inviting%20users%20and%20managing%20your%20team/Account-send_invitation.png?width=670&height=357&name=Account-send_invitation.png)

 

ℹ️  The invited user will receive an email with a link to join the account. The link can only be used once and **expires after 15 days.** If it expires, you'll need to resend the invitation.

### Managing pending invitations

To view invitations that are still awaiting acceptance, click the **pending invitations** button at the top of the Team tab. A panel will slide in showing each invitation's email, assigned role, and the date it was sent. [![Account-manage_invitations](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Inviting%20users%20and%20managing%20your%20team/Account-manage_invitations.png?width=670&height=357&name=Account-manage_invitations.png)](https://help.plytix.com/hubfs/Invitations-resend-remove-png.png)

---

Clicking **···** next to any pending invitation gives you three options:
- **Copy invitation link:** share the link manually instead of by email
- **Resend invitation: **send a fresh invitation email
- **Delete invitation: **cancel the invitation entirely

### Changing a user's role

You can change a user's role, or an invitee's role before they've accepted, at any time from the "**Team"** tab.  Choose from a set of pre-created [system roles](https://help.plytix.com/system-roles) or any [custom team roles](/team-roles-creation) you've created.

To change a user's role: 

****

| 1. Find the user or invitee whose role you'd like to change 
2. Click on the dropdown in the "Role" column 
3. Select the new role, it saves automatically |
| --- |

 

 

💡  You can also [assign roles to API keys.](https://help.plytix.com/api)

### Deleting an existing user

Owners and Admins can remove any user from the account, regardless of that user's role. Deleting a user removes their access to this account only, if they belong to multiple accounts, they must be removed from each one separately.

 

>

ℹ️  **Account owners cannot be deleted.** Ownership must be transferred to another user first.

 

To delete a user: 
1.

In the **Team** tab, hover over the user's row
2.

Click the **bin icon** that appears on the far right
3.

Type **DELETE** in the pop-up window
4.

Confirm your decision by clicking DELETE

⚠️  Deleting a user cannot be undone, but you can always re-invite them later.

### Transferring account ownership

Only the current account Owner can transfer ownership. This can be done at any time.

To transfer ownership of an account: 
1. In the **Team** tab, hover over the row of the user you'd like to make the new Owner
2. Click the **user icon** to the left of the bin icon
3. Confirm by clicking **TRANSFER OWNERSHIP**

 

[![account-transfer_ownership](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Inviting%20users%20and%20managing%20your%20team/account-transfer_ownership.png?width=670&height=357&name=account-transfer_ownership.png)](https://help.plytix.com/hubfs/Untitled%20design%20(2).png)

 

Ownership transfers immediately. The previous Owner is automatically reassigned the **Admin** role.

 

>

⚠️ Transferring ownership does **not** delete the previous owner. If you want to remove them from the account, you must do so manually.
