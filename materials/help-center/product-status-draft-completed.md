# Updating Product Status | Draft, Completed and Archived

## How to update the status of your product in Plytix

Product Status is a dropdown type [system attribute](https://help.plytix.com/system-attributes) in Plytix. It offers a quick way to know if your product is ready to be shared with the world. It is especially useful to filter products in [smart lists](https://help.plytix.com/create-and-manage-product-lists) for use in Channels and Catalogs. In this article you will learn how to modify your products' status - individually or in bulk.

### Changing the Status of Individual Products

 

To change the status of a product from the Product Overview page: 
1. Click on the product's status in the "Status" column
2. From the dropdown, choose the status you want to apply: '**Draft', 'Complete' **or '**Archived'.**

**

![Overview-status-edit](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Updating%20Product%20Status%20-%20Draft,%20Completed%20and%20Archived/Overview-status-edit.png?width=670&height=357&name=Overview-status-edit.png)

**

To change the Status of a product from the Product Detail page: 
1. From the Product Overview, click on the product SKU to enter that product's detail page
2. Click on the current status below the Product Label
3. From the dropdown, choose the status you want to apply: '**Completed', 'Draft' **or '**Archived'.**

![Product-details-edit-status](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Updating%20Product%20Status%20-%20Draft,%20Completed%20and%20Archived/Product-details-edit-status.png?width=670&height=357&name=Product-details-edit-status.png)

### Changing the Status of Products in Bulk

You can also change the status across a range of products from the Product Overview page. 

To do this: 
1. Click on '**Products'** in the top navigation menu
2. Select '**All products'** from the dropdown
3. Select the products you want to edit, click '**Edit products', **and then "Edit attributes"
[![Overview-bulk_edit-status-01](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Updating%20Product%20Status%20-%20Draft,%20Completed%20and%20Archived/Overview-bulk_edit-status-01.png?width=644&height=343&name=Overview-bulk_edit-status-01.png)](https://help.plytix.com/hubfs/Help%20center/Product%20Editing/Product%20status,%20draft%20or%20completed/bulk-select-products.png)
4. Choose the attribute '**Status'**
5. Set the new value and click '**Save'**
