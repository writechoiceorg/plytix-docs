# Exporting Relationships

## How to export relationships from the product overview page and from channels

There are two main ways to export data from Plytix: from the product overview table and from Channels. Channels provide more customization and more formatting options, while in the Product Overview, you can export spreadsheets and PDFs. 

[Exporting Relationships from the Product Overview](#export_relationships_from_product_overview)

[Exporting Relationships from Channels](#THISONE_export_relationships_from_channels)

_*Skip to a section in this article by clicking on the links above_

 

---

### Exporting Relationships from the Product Overview

1. In the Product Overview page, select the products you'd like to export.

2. Click the **'Export' **button at the bottom of the page

![overview-export](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Exporting%20Relationships/overview-export.png?width=670&height=357&name=overview-export.png)

3. Select your export method: **CSV**, **XLSX**, or **PDF**.

![overview-export-options](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Exporting%20Relationships/overview-export-options.png?width=670&height=357&name=overview-export-options.png)

4. Then select the elements you want to include. In the "Available Attributes" column, **"Relationships"** are located below **"System attributes."**

![overview-export-options-02](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Exporting%20Relationships/overview-export-options-02.png?width=670&height=357&name=overview-export-options-02.png)

5. Then click the purple **'Export'** button. 

If the file is small enough, it will **download automatically** in your browser. 

If it is a large file, you will get **an email with the file** once it has been processed. 

![overview-export-processing](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Exporting%20Relationships/overview-export-processing.png?width=670&height=357&name=overview-export-processing.png)

### Exporting Relationships from Channels

 

ℹ️  This guide will only discuss exporting **relationships **from an existing channel. To learn more about creating and setting up channels, check out this article: [Creating a Channel](https://help.plytix.com/creating-a-channel)

From the Channels tab, select the channel with the information you want to export by clicking on its name.

![channels-selector](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Exporting%20Relationships/channels-selector.png?width=670&height=357&name=channels-selector.png)

This brings you to the channel's page where you will see the **'Attributes'** tab below the name of the channel. Relationships are **added from here **and also** formatted here. **

**To add a relationship: **

1. Click the **'+ Add Attributes'** button.

![channel-attributes-add_attributes](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Exporting%20Relationships/channel-attributes-add_attributes.png?width=670&height=357&name=channel-attributes-add_attributes.png)

2. Select **'Relationships' **from the list below **'System Attributes' **and then select the relationships you want to export.

![channel-attributes-add_attributes-relationships](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Exporting%20Relationships/channel-attributes-add_attributes-relationships.png?width=670&height=357&name=channel-attributes-add_attributes-relationships.png)

3. Once selected, click the purple **'Add attributes'** button.

 

**Optional: Changing relationship export format**

1. Locate the relationship(s) in the list of attributes and click the **'Settings'** icon.

![channel-attributes-settings](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Exporting%20Relationships/channel-attributes-settings.png?width=670&height=357&name=channel-attributes-settings.png)

💡 Relationship attributes can be identified by the orange 'REL' label next to them.

 

2. In the panel that appears you can adjust the relationship output settings.

![channel-attributes-settings-02](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Exporting%20Relationships/channel-attributes-settings-02.png?width=670&height=357&name=channel-attributes-settings-02.png)

You can add, remove, and reorder by dragging and dropping: 
- Identifier
- Quantity separator
- Quantity

If you selected the option to "Show quantity separator," the separator field can't be empty: 

![channel-attributes-settings-03](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Help%20Center%20Images/HC%20-%20Exporting%20Relationships/channel-attributes-settings-03.png?width=670&height=357&name=channel-attributes-settings-03.png)

💡 The "RELATIONSHIP OUTPUT EXAMPLE" provides a preview of what your data will look like.

 
After clicking **'Okay'** in the Settings panel, you will need to process your channel to apply the new settings. Select **'Process now' **at the top of the main channel page.

**To export your file:**

Select **'Created or modified'** in the dropdown menu under "Product Processing" to make sure that all products get [processed](https://help.plytix.com/en/creating-a-channel#process) into the channel export. Then, click the purple **'Start processing'** button in the top right corner.

On the [main page](https://help.plytix.com/en/managing-channels#overview) of the channel, you'll see a link below the product name. You can click this link or click the **'Copy'** button and paste it into your internet browser to begin your download.

For a [CSV file](https://help.plytix.com/en/defining-data-format-for-scv-and-xlsx-feeds), you'll end up with something like this:

![Exporting Relationships.File-viewjpg](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Exporting%20Relationships/Exporting%20Relationships.File-viewjpg.jpg?width=670&height=209&name=Exporting%20Relationships.File-viewjpg.jpg)
