# Creating and Managing Product Families

## How to create and manage product families and use inheritance to automatically apply parent attributes to their variants

Product Families are a way for you to assign attributes to products of the same type so that you can see only relevant attributes. It is also a way for you to set consistent values across several products for the attributes you select through **automatic inheritance **from parent products to variants. Product families are especially useful if you have a variety of products in your catalog that share only a specific set of attributes.

###
Creating Product Families

To create a Product Family:
1. Navigate to '**Settings'** in the navigation bar.
2. In the left menu, under '**Products'**, choose the option '**Families'.**
3. Click '**+' **to create a new family

![Families - Settings](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Creating%20and%20Managing%20Product%20Families/New%20design%202026/Families%20-%20Settings.jpg?width=644&height=283&name=Families%20-%20Settings.jpg)

4. Give your family a name (you can change this later), choose the attributes that you want to be part of this family, and set inheritance. 

![Add family](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Creating%20and%20Managing%20Product%20Families/New%20design%202026/Add%20family.jpg?width=644&height=325&name=Add%20family.jpg)

In this section you can choose to turn the** inheritance ON **for the attributes you want. By enabling Inheritance ON at Level 1, attribute values from the parent will be reflected in all variants and sub-variants. Enabling it at Level 2 passes values from variants to their sub-variants. This is a great resource if all your variants share some common information with a higher-level product, for attributes such as description, for example.

            5.   Then Click **'Save'**

ℹ️ An Attribute can be assigned to more than one family.

Once you have created a Product Family, you can assign your products to a family. Learn [how to assign your products to a Product Family in this article](https://help.plytix.com/en/assigning-products-to-product-families).

###
Editing Product Families

To edit an already existing Product Family:
1. Navigate to '**Settings'** in the navigation bar.
2. In the left menu, under '**Products'**, choose the option '**Families'.**
3. Click on the name of the product family you wish to edit.
[![Families - Settings - Editing](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Creating%20and%20Managing%20Product%20Families/New%20design%202026/Families%20-%20Settings%20-%20Editing.jpg?width=644&height=137&name=Families%20-%20Settings%20-%20Editing.jpg)](https://help.plytix.com/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Create%20and%20Manage%20Product%20Families/edit-product-family.png)
4. If you wish to rename the Product Family, click on the name to edit it.
5. If you wish to add more attributes to this Family, click on '**Add attribute'**.

![Edit name - add attribute](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Creating%20and%20Managing%20Product%20Families/New%20design%202026/Edit%20name%20-%20add%20attribute.jpg?width=644&height=240&name=Edit%20name%20-%20add%20attribute.jpg)

6. If you wish to delete an attribute from this Family, select the attribute and click on '**Remove'**.

####
Editing an attribute's inheritance status

In Family Settings, you can switch the inheritance status ON/OFF for the attributes you select.

To change an attribute's inheritance status:

1. Select the attributes you want to change the inheritance status for

2. Click "Edit inheritance status"

3. Choose how you want your attributes to be inherited:
- Choose Inheritance ON (Level 1) if you want the attribute values to be passed on from the parents to variants and sub-variants
- Choose ON (Level 2) if you want the attribute values to be passed on from the variants to sub-variants

- Choose Inheritance OFF if you would like to keep the values at individual product levels

![Creating and Managing Product Families](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Creating%20and%20Managing%20Product%20Families/New%20design%202026/Creating%20and%20Managing%20Product%20Families.jpg?width=670&height=335&name=Creating%20and%20Managing%20Product%20Families.jpg)

⚠️ Keep in mind that choosing the** Inheritance status ON **may result in data loss, as the content will be passed on from parents to variants or sub-variants for the attributes with status ON.

ℹ️ Please note that only some [system attributes](https://help.plytix.com/en/system-attributes) can be inherited (Thumbnail, Label, Status and Categories)

4. Click** 'Save'. **

Within the Family overview you are also able to see which products are currently assigned to a Family:

![Family - products](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Creating%20and%20Managing%20Product%20Families/New%20design%202026/Family%20-%20products.jpg?width=670&height=275&name=Family%20-%20products.jpg)

ℹ️ From this view you are not able to edit which products are assigned to a family. If you want to learn [how to change or remove families from a product, check out this article](https://help.plytix.com/en/assigning-products-to-product-families).

### Duplicating and Deleting Product Families

If you want to duplicate or delete any existing Product Family, you can easily do so in a few clicks:
1. Navigate to '**Settings'** in the navigation bar.
2. In the left menu, under '**Products'**, choose the option '**Families'.**
3. Select the family you wish to duplicate.
4. Click on '**Duplicate' **or** 'Delete'**

![Delete Duplicate 1](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Creating%20and%20Managing%20Product%20Families/New%20design%202026/Delete%20Duplicate%201.jpg?width=670&height=336&name=Delete%20Duplicate%201.jpg)

⚠️ When duplicating Product Families, only the attributes will be duplicated, but not the products that have been assigned to the family.

Alternatively, you can go into the Family and select the option to duplicate or delete from the family detail area:

![Delete Duplicate 1 (2)](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Using%20Plytix/Creating%20and%20Managing%20Product%20Families/New%20design%202026/Delete%20Duplicate%201%20(2).jpg?width=670&height=306&name=Delete%20Duplicate%201%20(2).jpg)

ℹ️  When you delete a Family, all products in the Family are moved to the default "Unassigned" Family.
