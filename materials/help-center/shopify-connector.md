### Why use Plytix’s direct integration with Shopify

Connecting Plytix to Shopify is easy. If you’re getting started with a brand new Plytix account, it will be auto populated with product data from your Shopify store, so the setup takes only a few clicks. If you already have an existing Plytix account and would like to connect it to your Shopify store, you can do so by [creating a new Shopify channel](#create). 

**Here are some of the benefits of using Plytix’s Shopify integration:**
- Manage your product information and updates in Plytix, so that you have a centralized platform to refer to
- Create new products and sync updates into your store, with the option to have scheduled processing based on the frequency of your updates
- Create and sync metafields from Plytix
- Link digital files to your products and sync them into your store

In addition, you’ll have a dedicated Account Manager who will guide you through the setup of your channel to ensure your product information is sent correctly to your Shopify store.

To manage your Shopify product creation and updates directly from Plytix, [install the Plytix Shopify app](#install-app), which will connect your Plytix account with your Shopify store.

ℹ️ You can connect multiple Shopify stores to your Plytix account.

To get started, first, install the Plytix app in your Shopify account. Once you have installed it and connected your Plytix account to your selected stores, you may be required to [update the app](#update-app) according to changes released on Shopify’s end.

### Installing the Plytix Shopify app

Installing the Plytix Shopify app takes less than five minutes. Simply install it on [Shopify’s app store](https://apps.shopify.com/plytix-pim), and connect it to your Plytix account. If you already have a Plytix account, you can connect it to your store by [creating a new Shopify channel](https://help.plytix.com/en/shopify-connector). 

ℹ️ Note that you will need to install the app for every Shopify **store** you’d like to connect to Plytix. If you have different channels connected to the same store, you only need to install the app once.

⚠️ If you are currently using a Shopify channel that was previously connected to a custom app, you will see a banner in your channel informing you of the need to install the Plytix app to continue using the connection. Installing the app will not change your current channel setup nor will it modify your data. 

 

---

### Updating the Plytix Shopify app

Shopify performs periodic updates in their system that may modify their API requirements. Updates to the Plytix app are handled by our team to keep it up to date with new features and prevent loss of functionalities. However, some of those changes require action from the user’s end, such as access permission updates, for example.  

Whenever there is any action required on your end, a banner will appear in your Shopify channel. Please follow the steps on the message to maintain your channel’s functionalities. After you’ve completed the required action, you will receive a confirmation message. 

⚠️ If a banner appears in your channel, it is critical that you follow the steps to complete the required action. Failure to make the necessary updates can result in syncing errors, loss of current functionality, or prevent access to new features in your Shopify channel. Please act promptly to avoid disruptions.

ℹ️ If there is a pending app update and you have more than one **Shopify store** linked to your Plytix account, you will be required to update the app again for each **store** you have connected.

### Creating a Shopify Channel

The Shopify connector can be found in the Channels area, accessible via the **Channels** icon in the left sidebar. To create a new Shopify channel:
1. Go to the **Channels** icon in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Add Channel** button on the right.
3. Name your channel.
4. Choose the **Shopify** channel type.

![Create-shopify-channel](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/Updated%20Connection/Create-shopify-channel.jpg?width=670&height=364&name=Create-shopify-channel.jpg)

5. Choose either an existing connection or connect a new store

6. To connect a new store, click the **Open Plytix PIM app** button. This will redirect you to Plytix's app in the Shopify app store.

![add-new-shopify-store](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/Updated%20Connection/add-new-shopify-store.jpg?width=670&height=395&name=add-new-shopify-store.jpg)

 7. Once you're in the Shopify app store, click the **Install** button.

![plytix-shopify-app](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/Updated%20Connection/plytix-shopify-app.jpg?width=670&height=388&name=plytix-shopify-app.jpg)

ℹ️ You must be logged into your Shopify account to install the Plytix app.

8. After installing the app, log into your Plytix account.

![log-into-plytix-connector](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/Updated%20Connection/log-into-plytix-connector.jpg?width=670&height=625&name=log-into-plytix-connector.jpg)

9. Choose the account you would like to connect to Shopify.

 

![select-pim-account](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/Updated%20Connection/select-pim-account.jpg?width=597&height=510&name=select-pim-account.jpg)

10. Name your new channel and click the **Save** button. You can find your Shopify store's URL below the page.

![create-connected-channel](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/Updated%20Connection/create-connected-channel.jpg?width=670&height=363&name=create-connected-channel.jpg)

### Process Log

The process log shows the records of the last time information was pushed from Plytix to Shopify. If this is your first Shopify channel the log will be empty until you process it for the first time. 

[![Process Log 1](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/Process%20Log%201.jpg?width=670&height=282&name=Process%20Log%201.jpg)](https://help.plytix.com/hubfs/Help%20center/Channels/Shopify/shopify-channel-process-overview.png)

The log shows:
- **Start Date**: the date and time the process was started.
- **Finish Date**: the date and time the process completed.
- **Status**: whether the process is **Syncing**, **Processing**, **Canceled**, **Finished**, or **Skipped**.
- **Products**: how many products were processed.
- **Result**: whether the information was sent correctly, shown as **Success**; if not, a note with the number of errors the process had. 

ℹ️ A process shows as **Skipped** when a scheduled sync was due while the channel was still processing a previous run. That scheduled sync won't be queued to run later, if you still need that data synced, process the channel manually or wait for its next scheduled run.

ℹ️ Once you process a Shopify Channel, the processing is divided into two stages with the caching system: **processing** and **syncing**. During the processing stage, Plytix scans the products you are sending to your store and identifies those that are new or modified compared to the information stored in the cache. Then, new and modified products are synced into your Shopify and are cached for future reference. Learn more about processing a Shopify Channel [here](https://help.plytix.com/en/processing-a-shopify-channel).

Log Detail

If there are any errors in processing, you can check what happened at a product level by clicking the **Start Date** value of a process. This will take you to the log detail.

Here you will be able to review error messages from Shopify to remedy the issue. Click the **info** icon to see more information about the error.

In the log detail you can also see:
- **Action**: whether the product was **Created**, **Updated**, or **Skipped** (a product is Skipped after processing the channel, when it was already cached and hasn't experienced any changes since the last sync).
- **Result**: if a new or updated product was correctly processed, you will see the result as **Success**. If there were any errors with processing it to your store, the result will show as **Error**. If a product that was already cached is processed again with no updates to it, it will be skipped and thus the result will show up as blank.

![PROCESS LOG 2](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/PROCESS%20LOG%202.jpg?width=655&height=288&name=PROCESS%20LOG%202.jpg)

 

You can also download a report of your processing, either from the log detail page or by hovering over a specific process in the **Process Log** overview page.

ℹ️ Click the **info** icon to see a more detailed error message that tells you what exactly caused the error to happen.

**

![PROCESS LOG 3](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/PROCESS%20LOG%203.jpg?width=670&height=314&name=PROCESS%20LOG%203.jpg)

### Mapping

Here you will match attributes from Plytix to Shopify fields.

![Mapping (1)](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/Mapping%20(1).jpg?width=670&height=310&name=Mapping%20(1).jpg)

ℹ️ The fields in our connector are separated into two groups: Parent fields and Variant fields. However, this is simply for organizational purposes. If you sell simple products (products without variations) you may match attributes to any of the parent and variant fields, with the exception of [Option Name and Option Value](https://help.plytix.com/en/mapping-shopify-fields#options).

#### Shopify Fields

There are some key fields to verify to make sure your product information is correctly sent to your Shopify store:
- **Title** (required): is used to create your product handles if no custom handle is defined.
- **Handle:** when creating a new product from Plytix and the handle is either left blank or unmatched, the handle will be automatically generated based on the attribute you matched to the **Title** field.

As an additional reminder, always remember to make sure all of your **sellable products **have a value for their key attributes, like price, when you link them in the connector.

💡 Each Shopify field has certain requirements for formatting. [Use this guide](https://help.plytix.com/en/mapping-shopify-fields) to make sure you match the right content from Plytix to Shopify.

#### Mapping Attributes

To map a new attribute:
1. Click the **Match attribute** option in the **Plytix Attributes** column.
2. Select the attribute you want to match, or create an [Attribute Transformation](https://help.plytix.com/create-computed-attributes).
3. Then click the **Apply** button.
4. To save your changes, click the **Save changes** button in the save bar that appears at the top.

To unmatch, click the **Unmatch** icon.

![Unmatch](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/Unmatch.jpg?width=670&height=147&name=Unmatch.jpg)

#### Formatting Attributes

Just like with other channels in Plytix, you are able to make attribute transformations for each of your attributes. To do this:

1. Hover over the attribute you want to edit.

2. If it has transformation options available, a **Settings** icon will appear to the right. 3. Click the **Settings** icon, and all the options will appear.

![Creating and Managing a Shopify Channel-Attribute-Settings](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel-Attribute-Settings.jpg?width=670&height=198&name=Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel-Attribute-Settings.jpg)

4. Make your changes.

5. Click the **Apply** button. 6. Click the **Save Changes** button to save your transformations.

### Metafields

####

![Metafields](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/Metafields.jpg?width=670&height=228&name=Metafields.jpg)

There are two types of Shopify metafields: metafields with definition and metafields without definition.

#### To create a new metafield:

1. Click the **Create metafield** button, and a popup will appear.
2. Click **Definition** (selected by default) or **No definition**.
3. For metafields with definition, choose whether it is a **Product** or **Variant** metafield.
4. Type a namespace and key name, and select a value type. If creating a metafield with definition, type a definition name. It is optional to type a description. You can also select if you want to use the metafield in automated collections.
5. Click the **Create Metafield** button in the top right corner of the window.
6. After creating your metafield, you can match it to an existing attribute or create an attribute transformation to match with it by clicking the **Match metafield** option.
7. Select an attribute from the list or click the **Apply an attribute transformation** option.
8. Click the **Apply** button.
9. Click the **Save changes** button at the top of your channel to ensure the new metafield is saved.

💡 To learn more about metafields, including which value types are currently supported for metafields with and without a definition, [use this guide](https://help.plytix.com/en/shopify-metafields). Learn more about creating attribute transformations in [this article](https://help.plytix.com/en/create-computed-attributes).

#### Scheduling

To enable feed scheduling, click the toggle next to **Set up periodic processing for your feed**. This will open settings to configure when information is automatically sent to your store.

ℹ️ Note that enabling scheduling will automatically change your channel's status from **Draft** to **Live**, making it count towards your total number of live outputs.

### Duplicating a Shopify Channel

Whenever you need a similar setup for your store, but you don't want to create an entire Shopify Channel from scratch again, you can duplicate any existing Shopify Channel in Plytix. This is useful for testing a new setup before applying it to your live channel.

To do so:
1. Enter the Shopify Channel you wish to duplicate.
2. Click the **Duplicate** icon within the channel.

![duplicate-1](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/duplicate-1.jpg?width=670&height=342&name=duplicate-1.jpg)

This will duplicate the entire channel including the store connection and mapping options applied. 

💡 You can change the store connection in your duplicated channel. 

![Change connection](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/Change%20connection.jpg?width=670&height=327&name=Change%20connection.jpg)

⚠️ Keep in mind that changing your connection will clear out the cache for your channel, which will impact processing times. Learn more about how processing works [here](https://help.plytix.com/en/processing-a-shopify-channel).

![Connection.cache](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Sharing%20Your%20Data/Creating%20and%20Managing%20a%20Shopify%20Channel/Connection.cache.jpg?width=670&height=223&name=Connection.cache.jpg)

⚠️ If you keep the duplicate pointed at the **same** store as the original (for example, to test a mapping change before rolling it out), never process both channels into that store at the same time, doing so risks one overwriting the other's changes. Also make sure the **Handle** field stays mapped with values in both channels, since Handle is what keeps the connection matched to the right products.
