# Deleting Products

## How to delete an individual product, or delete many products at once in Plytix.

There are a few different ways to delete products in Plytix. You can delete a single product from the Product Overview table or from the Product Detail page. You can also delete many products at once from the Product Overview. 

⚠️  Be careful when deleting a product as this can't be undone. 

 

[Deleting a Single Product](#single-delete)

[Deleting Products in Bulk](#deleting_multiple_products_at_once)

### Deleting a Single Product

You can delete a single product from the Product Overview page or from its Product Detail page.

**To delete a product from the Product Overview:**
1. Using the search bar in the top right corner of the product overview, search for the product you wish to delete. 
        2.  Once you've found the product, select it and click the **'Delete'** icon.

![Deleting a Single Product](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Files%20Not%20Used%20in%20Articles/Product%20Editing/Delete%20products/New%20design%202026/Deleting%20a%20Single%20Product.png?width=670&height=323&name=Deleting%20a%20Single%20Product.png)

       3.   In the popup window type "DELETE" to confirm deletion, then click the red **'Delete' **button.

![DELETE CONFIRMATION](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Files%20Not%20Used%20in%20Articles/Product%20Editing/Delete%20products/New%20design%202026/DELETE%20CONFIRMATION.png?width=670&height=322&name=DELETE%20CONFIRMATION.png)

⚠️ If you are **deleting a parent**, all of its variants will be deleted too. If you wish to keep the variants as single products, make sure to [unlink them from the parent](https://help.plytix.com/en/manage-product-variations#unlink) first.

** To delete a product from its Product Detail page:**
1. Click on the product **SKU**. This will take you to that product's detail page, as pictured below. 
2. Click on the** '...'** icon on the left side of the page above the product thumbnail, then select **'Delete'**.

![Delete from Product Detail Page](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Files%20Not%20Used%20in%20Articles/Product%20Editing/Delete%20products/New%20design%202026/Delete%20%20from%20Product%20Detail%20Page.jpg?width=644&height=280&name=Delete%20%20from%20Product%20Detail%20Page.jpg)

3. You can also delete your products variants or sub-variants from the variants tab

![DELETE1](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Files%20Not%20Used%20in%20Articles/Product%20Editing/Delete%20products/New%20design%202026/DELETE1.jpg?width=644&height=303&name=DELETE1.jpg)

After selecting one or more variants and clicking **"Delete",** you'll need to confirm the action. The number of products shown in the confirmation will include the selected variants as well as any sub-variants underneath them,  if there are any.

![DELETE2](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Files%20Not%20Used%20in%20Articles/Product%20Editing/Delete%20products/New%20design%202026/DELETE2.jpg?width=644&height=386&name=DELETE2.jpg)

  4. In the popup window, type "DELETE" to confirm you want to delete this product, then click              the red** 'Delete' **button.

Please note that **whenever a parent product is deleted**, all of **its variants will be deleted **too.

Deleting Products in Bulk

You can delete anywhere from a few to all products at once from the product overview table. 

**To do this: **
1. Click on **'Products'** in the navigation menu.
2. In the product overview table, select all the products you want to delete by clicking on the checkbox to the left of the products' SKU.

💡 Use [filters](https://help.plytix.com/filtering) to narrow your product search or pull up a particular set of products. 

       3.   Click on the **'Delete'** button that appears at the bottom of the product overview table.

![Delete - in bulk](https://help.plytix.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Help%20center/Files%20Not%20Used%20in%20Articles/Product%20Editing/Delete%20products/New%20design%202026/Delete%20-%20in%20bulk.jpg?width=670&height=329&name=Delete%20-%20in%20bulk.jpg)

       4.   In the popup window, confirm your selection by typing "DELETE", then click the red                           **'Delete'** button.

⚠️ If you've selected parent products to delete, all of their variants will be deleted too.
